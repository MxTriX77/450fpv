The analog PAL goggles feed and the OSD rendering (tech-artist, phase 4).

- `Core/` (`Fpv450.Feed.Core`, engine-free): `FeedEvents` and `FeedLoss` ported from the Godot feed, `FeedFont` (the OSD's character ROM and its atlas) and `FeedOsd` (the 30 × 16 grid's geometry and its codes, the receiver's two text lines in rows 16–17) ported from the Godot OSD, `StubOsd` (the Godot OSD's layout with invented values, the labelled stand-in until the OSD content model), `IFeedSignals` with `StubFeedSignals`, `FeedUniforms` (the pass's per-field values, and the amps and dB scales), and `FeedSchedule` (the schedule dump and digest). `tools/feedcore` compiles the same files, `tools/feedbench schedule --seed <n>` dumps a seed's schedule, and `tools/feedtests` runs `Tests/EditMode/Feed/` outside Unity.
- `FpvCamera` (the rig, on the FPV camera): the pinned intrinsics and the reduced render with the lens's extra field of view, a child output camera that carries the feed to the screen, the 50 fields/s clock, and the OSD's codes (uploaded once per field from `IFeedSignals.OsdCell`) and glyph atlas. `FeedSettings` (`Settings/Feed/Resources/FeedSettings.asset`) holds its numbers.
- `NoclipFeed`: puts the rig on the test range's noclip camera when that scene loads, with `NoclipFeedSignals` (the camera's measured angular rate, every other signal a labelled stub), the V toggle, the O review overlay and the `-feed`, `-feedwidth` and `-feedcost` options (`docs/test-range.md`).
- `FeedFeature` (on every URP renderer): `Shaders/Feed.shader`, the Godot `video_feed.gdshader` ported, drawn on cameras with a `FeedOutput` only.
- `Editor/`: `FeedSetup.Apply` re-derives the settings asset, the renderer features and `Scenes/FeedTest.unity`; `FeedCapture.Stills -feedField a-b[+cut|+hardcut][,...]` is the Unity side of the Godot feed's `--video-field` (with `-feedInput frame.png` for the A/B's identical input frame), and `FeedCapture.Lens` is the lens scenario.
