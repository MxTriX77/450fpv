## 4. Findings to keep
- **Micro-motion (D-064, D-068):**
  - At 1–4 Hz it is now inside the pilot's footage, and lower hovers are livelier.
  - At 4–15 Hz it is 1.5–2× over the footage, so T6d pitch fails. The cause is the prop's thrust spectrum shape (Shetty & Selig 2011, APC 10×5).
  - A hover's yaw is too still: the harness pilot's stick noise needs a floor.
- **Pitch is still about 2.4× low in Severe:** the rotor interference adds 20 %. Next candidates: the body's lift and the bottle's Munk moment, and the edgewise thrust gust sensitivity.
- **Power (D-066, D-069):**
  - The mass build matches the class within 2–4 %: 4.09 kg ready to fly without a bottle.
  - The ESCs limit no current, giving 14.2 kgf.
  - The hover takes 37 / 45 / 53 % of the stick with a 0 / 1 / 2 kg bottle on the pilot's flown curve.
  - The punch-out shake is the wash's, 0.1–3.4°.
- **Landing (D-067):**
  - On hard ground the law matches rigid-body mechanics.
  - The rest of the pilot's "falls more in real life" comes from three things: the ground catching a skid (`p7a`'s grooves), airmode holding the drone up while armed (a G7 question), and the build's centring (the `build.placement` tolerance now gives about 1 mm).
- **Footage (`docs/reference-notes/ground-micro-motion.md`):**
  - Clip S's behaviour and the near-ground hovers are measured.
  - No clip shows an arming, a liftoff or a touchdown; the pilot has none.
  - The recorders show fields at 2-2-1 steps.
- **Known open physics bugs:**
  - Post-crash tunnels: a leg or an arm through a thin rigid rod (crown bark, a wire) at 5–13 m/s. Fixed by `p7f`'s breakage, a bark cantilever law or `p7d`'s soft wires.
  - The trench (E01) is too windy inside: the cavity model needs the lip height from the world builder.
- **Auto mode's safety check** has blocked five agents mid-task. Never run a blocked agent's commands; the pilot approved fresh agents from the files on disk.
