# Gap review: the Godot OpenSpec documents against the Unity docs

A fresh-context review (D-034, D-035) by the QA Engineer, who did none of the work reviewed. It lists what the frozen Godot attempt's OpenSpec documents require, and what its lessons teach, that the Unity docs don't carry yet, and says where each item belongs. The orchestrator folds the items into the Unity docs through a chore PR; this file changes nothing else.

## What was compared

- **Godot side:** all 50 files under `openspec/` at `godot-final` (read with `git show`, never checked out): the project config; 11 capability specs; every proposal, design, tasks list and pilot-answers file; and the 7 reviews (`define-map-format` QA, api and surfaces reviews and orchestrator check; `study-flight-references` and `study-wind-reference` QA; `bootstrap-godot-project` QA; `build-uat1-parts` terrain review). For lessons: `docs/knowledge.md` and `docs/legacy/` (flight-model specs, map-and-world reviews, the feed port spec, the old decision log and last report).
- **Unity side:** `docs/requirements.md`, `docs/plan.md`, `docs/asset-list.md`, `docs/pilot-brief-2026-10-17.md`, `VISION.md` (read only), `docs/decisions.md` (D-001 to D-036), `docs/pilot-questions.md`, `docs/environment.md`, the object sheets where a row points at them, `openspec/specs/*/spec.md` and the seven open changes under `openspec/changes/` (not the archive).
- **Unique Godot items.** The six synced specs under `openspec/specs/` are byte-for-byte the archived deltas apart from their headers, so each requirement is counted once: **62 requirements and 108 scenarios** in 11 capabilities.

**How an item was judged:**
- **Covered:** a Unity requirement, decision, plan step, open change or object sheet carries it, or a requirement cites the legacy copy that carries it (for example WLD-11 citing K§10 and `docs/legacy/map-and-world/world-query-spec.md`), and no Unity decision changes its premise.
- **Partly covered:** carried in part, or carried by a pointer while a Unity decision (the frame, Unity Terrain, a new tool set) changes what it means.
- **Missing:** no Unity requirement, decision, plan step, open change or sheet carries it. Text that survives only in a `docs/legacy/` copy that no requirement cites for it counts as missing.
- **Priority:** **P1** blocks G1 or G2, or touches the pilot's four emphasis items; **P2** is needed before a later gate (G3 to G8); **P3** is nice to have.

## Pilot emphasis

**1. The feed's realism.** Covered well: every measured trait of K§11 is a FEED requirement, and that includes the noise (N1 grain, N2 sparkles, N3, N4, quality drops), the glitches (N8 tear, the tells), the flashes (N12 recovering dropouts with a clean recovery), the staged loss and hard cut with near-binary snow, and night as the low-light end of the same pipeline with N13 dimming the OSD. The pilot's "colour that changes as the grass comes closer" is C2 AWB with C1 and C3 (FEED-04), judged at FEED-12 only by AE and AWB time constants. Not covered:
- the measured approach shifts themselves (L3)
- the scene-side colour continuity from near grass to far ground (L4)
- the FPV camera's field of view and aspect, which every side-by-side depends on (L1)
- a tool that measures the Unity output with the same per-frame metrics as the footage, without which FEED-12's statistics can't be compared (G13)

**2. The Godot noise port (D-026).** FEED-00 and the port spec in `docs/legacy/video-feed-godot/` fix the algorithms, constants, hash, seeded `System.Random`, rates, loss sequences and OSD geometry. The acceptance diffs the event schedule over 15,000 fields and matches stills per stage. Not covered: running the original needs the Godot build and a `godot-final` checkout, which `docs/environment.md` retires; and the still comparison should use one identical input frame in both pipelines, since the two scenes differ (L2).

**3. Controller binding and calibration.** The Godot OpenSpec set has no input capability (bootstrap made it a non-goal; only D-016 existed), so this review adds nothing from it. The Unity side already holds:
- INP-01 to INP-07: TX12 HID, the wizard (channel order, reversal, endpoints, deadband, arm switch, live bars, profiles, the tiny-span health check), Betaflight 4.5 ACTUAL rates and the throttle curve with the sim defaults from fc-profiles, room for more settings, the timing contract and the disconnect failsafe.

What it lacks, outside the Godot set:
- G16's physical-key binding for noclip on Ukrainian layouts.
- Two observations for the orchestrator. INP-03 and PHB-03 don't name airmode or motor idle (fc-profiles records `min_throttle 1070`), which decide what "throttle to zero at touchdown" does in PHY-02. Nothing detects expo or dual rates left on the radio, which would stack with the sim's rates.

**4. The physics list.** Covered by PHY-01 to PHY-25 and K§6 to K§8:
- prop wash (PHY-08 ground effect and recirculation; PHY-09 settling into the drone's own wash)
- turbulence (PHY-13 Dryden layers and wakes)
- gusts (PHY-13a, T7)
- wobble (T1 to T12, PHY-07 asymmetry and Calm's floor)
- bounded randomness, never noise on the pose (PHY-24, PRD-08)
- the tether (PHY-18 to PHY-21)
- wires (PHY-22)
- contacts (PHY-01 to PHY-06, PHB-05's latch)

Not covered:
- the physics over terrain holes (G7)
- the forward-flight thrust derate and the T9b watch item (L11)
- the wind statistics tools the targets need (G15)
- the testable scenarios that live only in the legacy flight-model specs (L10)
- the deferred X-items (L8)
