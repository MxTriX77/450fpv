## Findings that matter for phase 1
- **Unity Hub must be running for batchmode.** The MSIX Hub keeps the Personal licence in its sandbox; without it, Unity exits 198. Documented.
- **`git lfs checkout` in a new worktree.** Worktrees can hold LFS pointers; Unity then imports nothing and the capture still exits 0. Documented; the capture should fail loudly (tech-artist follow-up).
- **K01's material:** a lone brick needs one brick cell of the texture, not a wall tile (asset-artist, first phase-1 task on K01). Roughness and AO are imported but not wired: phase 1 derives a packed mask map by script.
- **Before the second asset:** a params sanity check and the automated collision-vs-mesh audit in `build.py` (physics review P1-2); cylinder colliders in the importer (P1-1); `lodBias` per quality level (tech-artist).
- **Capture:** the far-side scale bar hides behind assets taller than about 1.2 m; amend the spec at the first house.
- **Hygiene:** map rasters under `maps/` need a media-rule exemption in the phase-3 map-format change; the reference tools could look clips up by letter so nobody types a file name.
- **Four commit subjects over 72 characters** reached the trunk; the `commit-msg` hook now enforces the limit (`enforce-commit-format`, merging after its re-review).
