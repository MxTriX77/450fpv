## 3. Decisions and rulings this session
- **D-037** (above). The parity criterion for vegetation LODs is **"No LOD switch pops"**: at each switch the incoming LOD against the outgoing one, at 50 m the shown LOD against LOD0, within 6 % luminance and 15 % coverage, day and overcast, each value the mean over 64 sub-pixel offsets (256 where the 64 leave an error at or over 1 %, task 1.2k) with its standard error under 1 %. LOD0 is not a reference past its own switch (its sub-pixel leaves drop out of the raster).
- The review stage lifts an asset only by `max(0, -min.y)` (L01 task 1.6).
- Physics material rows are per touching part (`p1-materials-per-part`).
- D-038, D-039 (above).
- Hole assets (trench, cellars, dugouts) keep every cavity face 0.65 m inside their bands and their backing 0.75 m past them; WLD-16's cell-marking rule stays the world-builder's in phase 3. A record's band is every point within half its width of its centre line.
- The thin-wire material is chosen by the metadata's per-LOD `thin_wire` flag, never by uv2.
