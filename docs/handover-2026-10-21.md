## 2. Architecture as built (the pieces the next session works with)

**Asset pipeline (D-004: rebuilt, never hand-edited)**
- The flow: `assets-src/<class>/<variant>.json` params → `tools/blender/build.py` and its generator (`generators/`) → FBX plus `<variant>.assetmeta.json` → Unity's `CatalogImporter` and `ModelPostprocessor` → prefab, materials, `AssetPhysicsMeta` → `ReviewCapture` review sheets.
- One command: `pwsh tools/build_asset.ps1 <ID>`.
- **Generators:**
  - shrub (V13), tree (V01), pole (L01), wire_span (L03), trench (E01)
  - the S04 ground patch with `lib/straw`, K01 bricks
  - on the R01 branch: vehicle and debris_car
- **Libraries (`tools/blender/lib`):**
  - `meta`: the contract and validator
  - `audit`: collision follows the visual
  - `export`, `geometry`
  - `skeleton`: tubes, Leonardo's rule, bark patches, the shrub thicket
  - `cards`: leaf cards, thinning by facing
  - `shell`: crown shells, rules (a)–(d)
  - `impostor`
  - `holes`: F-1…F-8
  - `strip`: wall-space strips
  - `straw`
  - `loft` (R01 branch)
- **Textures:** `tools/assets/derive.py` derives them from CC0 sets pinned in `tools/assets/manifest.json`. `CREDITS.md` is generated from it.

**Metadata contract (`openspec/specs/asset-pipeline`)**
- bounds; LODs with tris, switch distance and the required `thin_wire` flag; cull
- material slots: a visual recipe plus a physics id from `assets-src/physics_materials.json`, one row per touching part, surfaces as `surface:<id>`
- primitives (box, sphere, capsule, cylinder; `convex` planned for F01)
- gaps (planar polygons with `narrowest_m`), wind volumes, snag records
- `ports` and `wires` (L01/L03)
- `holes`: a centre line, a band of half its width around it; one per cut and one per niche; ids local to the asset
- `textures` (the `crack_mask` role, on the R01 branch)

**Rules that the audits enforce**
- the planted rule (buried surface not audited)
- the wire audit at min(5 mm, D/2), with the reach check
- the hole contract F-1…F-8:
  - fillers 0.3 m thick, overlapping 0.2 m (checked at build for every asset with `holes`)
  - every cavity face 0.65 m inside the bands; backing 0.75 m past them
  - grassed tops are `belt_straw`, never tied with another top
- the shell rules for crowns

**Look**
- **Shaders:**
  - `Leaf` with `CoverageAlpha.hlsl` (true coverage at every mip)
  - `Impostor`: the normal atlas holds the unnormalised mean of the geometric normals and occlusion in alpha; each texel is lit as a von Mises–Fisher spread of normals; BC7, nearest-texel reads
  - `ThinWire`: analytic coverage, a round shadow caster
  - `CrazedGlass` (R01 branch); `FacadeLayered` is H01's, planned
- **The LOD parity measure** `Fpv450.LodParity.Measure`: "No LOD switch pops". At each switch the incoming LOD against the outgoing one, at 50 m the shown LOD against LOD0, within 6 % luminance and 15 % coverage, day and overcast. The grid is 8 × 8 sub-pixel offsets, 16 × 16 where the error reaches 1 %.
- **The review rig:** `ReviewCapture`, day and overcast at sun 45°, with an earthwork framing. Its GPU-upload leak fix (`f08fdb8`) and legend wrap are on the V01 branch only, until V01 merges.

**References**
- `reference/` is local only. Letters A–Q and S; R is skipped (it means "research only" in the sheets).
- `tools/reference/extract_frames.py` and `contact_sheet.py` write into `reference/_frames/<letter>/`; the letter map is `reference/_frames/letters.md`.
- Comparison sheets live only in `reference/_compare/`.

**The sim core** (`unity/Packages/com.fpv450.sim`, engine-free) was not touched this session.
