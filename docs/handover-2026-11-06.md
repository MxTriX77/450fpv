## 4. Findings to keep

- **The lag (D-087).** The cause is the 1 kHz step overrunning real time, so the sim falls behind and the controls feel late (it reads as RX loss).
  - In .NET under load, inside the house (`flightbench --bench houses-window-step`): p50 about 126 µs, p99 about 295 µs, 0 B.
  - Earlier in the player (Mono), steps measured 400–1000 µs. **The in-player step time has not been measured since the cuts.**
  - What `FlightScheduler` does when it falls behind (a catch-up pass) decides whether the pilot feels slow motion, late controls or a stall. Check it against the pilot's videos.
  - The fiber's queries are now the largest world share. A proposed `StaticContactsNear` API is not built; it needs a decision that supersedes D-041.
  - The per-thread `RegionCache` (17 KB on the flight thread's first step) still needs the physics engineer's sign-off.
- **R:** a new seed misses the wake-grid cache (30–40 ms). An optional prefetch of the next seed's grid at flight start would bring it to about 15 ms.
- **The climb:** τ of 1–2 s is the physics for a 4–6 kg drone on four 10-inch props. Body lift (Aero) is the next candidate.
- **Fiber reverses** (the 1.11 note on the line):
  - On the session drone, sharp reverses cut 26/50 in Calm at 36–100 km/h, all at the rear discs.
  - The scenario pilot flares to 52–58° and sinks 1–1.7 m/s. At the reversal the payout stops, the line goes slack (0.003–0.015 N) and a rear blade strikes it near the tip.
  - The edge peaks at 0.69–1.49 GPa against a strength of 3.0–5.0 GPa.
  - The pilot says the line tears at the hole's edge: not met.
  - Windy 100 km/h cruise: 1/60 tears at the edge on the session drone.
  - Open spikes: 18.6 N on the span (Calm 100 km/h, seed 3) and 12.40 N for one step at the exit.
- **The arm** (`docs/research/arm-plates-and-blade-loss.md` on the line): within the published ranges except the damping (0.02 against 0.004–0.013) and the 2 % linear shear break (woven 0/90 reaches 5 %). No published test exists of a full-blade loss in flight or of a 10-inch arm crash.
- **The canister:** a clear shell would be PETG or PC, about 20 % heavier than the ABS in the mass note (unchanged). The shell casts no shadow.
- **Tooling:** `tools/feedbench` doesn't build (`GoldenFlights` isn't linked; make it partial). QA still owes a review of the `FlightScheduler` stop-between-steps change.
