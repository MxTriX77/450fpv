## 4. Findings to keep

- **Yaw was slow from a controller bug:** Betaflight's ×2.5 yaw I. It is fixed: t90 is about 150 ms where it was 255–273. Roll and pitch track like the class. The pilot's real FpvPrep profile (rate profile 0: 50/220/0.45, yaw 80/240/0.20) is gentler than the old sim default. If yaw still feels soft, the OTU builder's yaw tune (P 45, I 30, F 0) is the gentlest in the fleet; the Beshket configs fly P 50–80, I 55–150, F 45–100. That is a pilot choice.
- **Liftoff** with the pilot's saved `thr_mid` 78 and `thr_expo` 36: 33 / 39 / 44 % stick at 0 / 1 / 2 kg, against 40 / 46 / 52 % before. The pilot may want less ("a little bit").
- **Landings:** BLHeli_S and Bluejay brake a disarmed motor by default (damped light). Disarmed touchdowns now tip at 0.01–0.11 m/s less drift. Soft PVC-U legs with P·Δ tip at about 14.4° of roll (rigid 23.3°).
- **The armed tip-over slide** (soil carve): airmode holds about 57° at full motors and slides about 21 m at 13 m/s. It is being fixed on `--tip`.
- **Legs' sway** after a windy landing lasts about 4 s at 2 Hz. The joints' friction doesn't slip at sway amplitudes. QA to judge; soil damping is untested.
- **Pitch is still about 2.4× low in Severe.** The real blade inertia lifted it only 2–3.5 %.
- **The OSD throttle** reads the stick before the curve, as Betaflight 4.5 does. The pilot's sample shows a small ▲ that no stock font has; the pilot was asked and hasn't answered.
- **Asset pipeline:** S04 and D01 rebuild byte-identically. The importer keeps `_MainTex` on base maps (fix `c86b55ed` is on the player only; bring it to the line with `--s04`).
