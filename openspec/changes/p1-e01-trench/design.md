# Design: p1-e01-trench

## Context
The pipeline after `p1-s04-belt-straw` and `p1-l01-l03-pole-wires`: `lib/ground_patch` (a gridded patch with relief, mounds, the S04 material, the straw kit baked as micro meshes), the `ground` recipe kind with a detail layer, micro meshes skipped by the audit, `surface:<id>` on shapes, the surface framing of the capture, `ports`, `wires`. The sheet §E01 and the group's shared rules give every number: lip 0.8 m, floor 0.6 m, depth 1.5 m, batter 86°, berm 0.3 m, parapets 0.45 m (field side) and 0.3 m (belt side) high on 2.5 m bases at 22° (derived from a 1.05 m²/m cut × 1.25 swell), bends 25° every 5 m (G), niches 0.8 × 0.6 × 0.7 m one per 10 m, topsoil band 0.5 m, lip crumble 0–0.1 m, colours #4a463f (inside, shade), #b49c6a (parapet straw), #a88f70 (subsoil band), the hole contract F-1…F-8 (on 0.5 m cells the band runs 0.65 m past the cavity's widest outline and the backing 0.75 m past the band's edge, as the cellar C01's), the width-at-height table. Labels below are for the numbers this design adds.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** the G trench on a self-contained patch, with fillers and gaps the physics will read unchanged, and a `holes` record the map compiles later.
**Non-Goals:** see proposal; no heightfield of its own (the patch is a mesh), no blending into neighbouring surfaces (the patch's margins end at its edge; the gallery floor meets them).

## Decisions
1. **Patch and run.** `run_m` 24 (assumed: three G bends and both niches fit; the 10 m tile shows the whole run), the centre line a polyline from `shape` curving with `bend_deg` 25 every `bend_every_m` 5 (G), seeded jitter ±5° and ±1 m; the patch is the run's bounding box plus `margin_m` 6 on every side (derived: the parapet base 2.5 m plus the berm plus 3 m of plain ground), built on `lib/ground_patch` at 0.25 m with S04's relief and material, refined to 0.1 m within 0.6 m of the lips (derived: a 0.1 m crumble needs 0.1 m cells). Origin at the first centre-line point, y = 0 the ground.
2. **The cut.** Per segment between centre-line samples (1 m straight, 0.5 m on bends, assumed so that planar wall faces stay within 5 cm of the curve): the floor at −`depth_m`, walls at `batter_deg` 86 from the lip width to the floor width, the lip crumbled by a seeded 0–0.1 m inset with 0.2–0.3 m spade facets only in the normal map; two niches cut into one wall at `niche_every_m` 10 (sill 0.15 m above the floor). The berm is level for `berm_m` 0.3 from each lip, then the parapet rises on a 22° slope to its height and falls on the far side; `age` 0.8 gives grassed parapets (S04's material, lodged) and a trampled floor; `age` 0 (not built) gives bare lumpy spoil (S03's look, assumed) and sharp lips.
3. **Floor detail.** Spilled clods: `clods_per_m` 0.5–2 (assumed from G's floor reading lumpy), 0.1–0.4 m, as low irregular meshes with one `surface:crater_spoil` sphere each, within 5 cm; blown-in straw and twigs at a tenth of S04's densities (assumed) as micro meshes from `lib/straw`, so the audit skips them.
4. **Fillers.** Per segment: a floor box 0.3 m thick under the floor plane; two wall boxes 0.3 m thick behind each wall face, rotated to the batter, from the floor to the lip; two lip boxes 0.3 m thick under the berm, level (F-3); each extends 0.1 m past its segment's ends so neighbours overlap 0.2 m (F-5) and the niche walls get their own three boxes; every cell the bands can mark is sealed from below by a filler (F-1, a 5 cm ray grid over the bands and 0.71 m past their edges: a cell marked by its centre reaches at most 0.354 m past a band, one marked by touch 0.707 m, so the lip boxes and the backing run 0.75 m past the band's edge, under the berm and the parapets' inner slopes and never above y = 0), and every cavity face, the crumbled lip and the niches included, stands at least 0.65 m inside the union of the bands (F-2: 0.25 m plus half a 0.5 m cell's diagonal, 0.354 m, rounded up, so it stays 0.25 m inside the hole cells at any placement yaw whichever way a cell is marked); nothing above the patch's ground outside the cut (F-4: the lip boxes top at y = 0). Materials: `surface:belt_bare` on every cut face (the sign-off's F-7 row), not landable. Parapets: per segment one tilted box per slope face (rotated 22° about the run direction, 0.3 m thick) and one flat box for the crest, `surface:belt_straw`, landable; margins: the patch's flat box from S04 (12 m-style, here the patch's extent), `surface:belt_straw`, landable. The audit's near-zone rule (2 cm at landable faces) applies to the lips and parapets: relief there is held to ±2 cm (assumed; S04's ±4 cm holds only on the plain margins).
5. **Gaps.** A cross-section polygon at every centre-line sample and at every bend vertex: the clear trapezoid from the floor to the lip in the plane normal to the run; the top slot per segment: the quad between the two lips at y = 0. `narrowest_m` is the inscribed circle (0.75 m for the default section, derived: the circle touches both walls where the width equals its diameter), and the design records that the drone's verdicts come from the sheet's width-at-height table (nose-first everywhere, yawed in the top 0.7 m), which the handoff states and the gap probe of step 8 flies. Gap ids carry the chainage (`xs_12.0`, `slot_12`) so the probe's log names the place.
6. **The `holes` record** (`lib/meta.py`, additive, schema stays 1): `{"id": "run", "centre_line": [...], "top_width_m": 0.8, "depth_m": 1.5, "band_width_m": 2.3}` (derived: the lip at its widest, 0.8 m plus the 0.1 m crumble a side, plus 0.65 m a side, C01's margin), and one record per niche in the same form: its centre line square to the run from the trench's centre line to the niche's back wall, `top_width_m` 0.8, `depth_m` 1.35 (the niche's floor), `band_width_m` 2.1 (derived: a niche's cavity lies under the ground up to 0.7 m past the wall, and ground over a cavity must be a hole, as over C01's chamber, or the terrain contact takes the drone inside it for a penetration; the asset's own ground and the niche's ceiling box carry the ground there). A record's band is every point within `band_width_m` / 2 of its centre line in plan. The map compiler (phase 3) rasterises the band into `holes.png` and lowers nothing (the fillers carry the cut); the parapets become heightfield in the world (F-7), which the compiler reads from the asset's mesh there, not from this record.
7. **Materials.** `trench_wall.json`, kind `ground`, in wall space (u along the run, v down the wall): base = the chosen loam set under `brown_mud_02`, tinted to #4a463f, with a wall mask (the topsoil band #33302b in the top `topsoil_m` 0.5, a root fringe in the top 0.2 m, clod spill #8a7660 in the bottom 0.2 m) composed into a 2048 × 256 strip (85 × 170 px/m, derived from the 24 m run; the tiled detail layer at 512 px/m carries the grain), and a spade-facet normal (0.2–0.3 m facets, < 2 cm) derived by script; the floor uses the same recipe's floor row (trampled, #4a463f). The parapets and margins: `belt_straw` as is (superseded by task 1.4: used as is it stamps S04's bare-patch ovals every 12 m with no relief under them; the parapets get their own grassed-spoil recipe, the physics staying `surface:belt_straw`).
8. **LODs.** LOD0 everything (grid, clods, micro); LOD1 at 8 m without micro; LOD2 at 60 m the patch at 1 m with the cut as a plain slot; cull 400 m (assumed, as S04). Budget: under 60 000 triangles at LOD0 for the ground, the lip refinement and the clods (derived: the 0.25 m patch is 15 000, the lip refinement about 5 000, the clods under 2 000), plus the lodged-mat micro bake under 450 000 as S04's (S04 design 4: a stand-in for AST-10's near ring; amended after QA's R1, task 1.6), drawn on the LODs the review's 1.5 m and 10 m views use.
9. **Review capture.** `ReviewCapture`: for catalog class `earthworks` with a `holes` record, the pilot-height camera stands 1.5 m above the lip at the first centre-line point (offset 0.4 m to the field side), looks along the run's first segment and 20° down (assumed: G flies along the trench at a low height), with the bar planted 3 m along the run beside the cut; the pose is printed. K01 and S04 captures stay pixel-identical.
10. **Params file** (`assets-src/earthworks/E01_curving.json`): `seed`, `shape` curving, `run_m` 24, `bend_deg` 25, `bend_every_m` 5, `top_w_m` 0.8, `bottom_w_m` 0.6, `depth_m` 1.5, `batter_deg` 86, `berm_m` 0.3, `parapet_h_m` {field 0.45, belt 0.3}, `parapet_base_m` 2.5, `parapet_slope_deg` 22, `niche_every_m` 10, `niche_m` {w 0.8, h 0.6, d 0.7}, `lip_crumble_m` 0.1, `topsoil_m` 0.5, `age` 0.8, `clods_per_m` 1, `margin_m` 6, `revetment` none, `collapse` 0, `overgrowth` 0, `lod` { 8, 60, 400 }; every key with a range and an effect in `PARAMS`; `check` refuses a floor wider than the lip, a batter under 75°, and parapet volume that does not match the cut's swell within 30 %.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- [Overlapping filler boxes confuse the two-sided audit near the overlaps] → the audit measures open faces only; the design states which face of each box is open; the audit lines show it.
- [Tilted parapet boxes over a crumbled grassed slope] → the slope is planar per segment at 22°; the relief on it is held to ±2 cm (decision 4).
- [The strip map's 85 px/m along the run reads soft at 1.5 m] → the tiled detail carries the grain; judged against G, where the inside is in shade and reads uniformly dark.
- [The zigzag and straight shapes are untested] → range-checked only; built with E02–E04 later.

## Open Questions
- Which loam set (`dirt_floor` or `forest_ground_04`) reads as G's shaded walls: decided at the comparison sheet.
- **Settled: the band's margin against the map grid.** C01's rule, which holds whether the compiler marks a cell by its centre or by touch: every cavity face 0.65 m inside the bands, the backing 0.75 m past their edges, a band per niche (decisions 4 and 6). WLD-16's marking rule stays the world-builder's choice in phase 3 and changes nothing here. The berm stays its look value (`berm_m` 0.3): the asset draws its own ground, berm and the parapets' inner slopes over the hole cells, and the heightfield takes the parapets from its mesh outside them (decision 6), so a wider band widens no berm. The question as it was put (found by the cellar sheets' holes pass): a 0.5 m margin holds F-2 (cavity faces at least 0.25 m inside the hole cells) only when the run is aligned with the grid; at 45° with cells marked by their centre a face comes within 0.146 m of the cell boundary. Either the margin grows to at least 0.61 m (0.25 m plus half a 0.5 m cell's diagonal; C01 and C02 use 0.65 m), which takes the band past the shared rules' 2.0 m, or the compiler marks every cell the band touches and the backing fillers reach 0.71 m past the band. Settled with WLD-16's cell-marking rule (world-builder, phase 3) before task 1.1 writes the record; the record's field stays the same either way.
