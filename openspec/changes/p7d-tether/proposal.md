## Why
The pilot's manifesto puts the fiber at the centre of how they fly: smooth orbits, never a sharp reverse, and a line that "may (or may not)" cut. They named what cuts it in practice: "overly sharp reverse, snagging on trees, very sharp lift off, or sudden momentum backwards after lift off" (PHY-19). Today the coil is a mass that never changes, the feed's link signals are stubs, and nothing is attached to the drone. After flying the map build the pilot also asked for wires that behave like the real ones, which "get tangled, twisted and flipped ... just like in real life coz they are smooth, they are not solid textures, they are soft and a bit elastic" (D-059); today every conductor is a rigid capsule chain (1.6b), so a strike is a crash and nothing else. Both are lines: one solver serves them, and every outcome must come from the line's mechanics, the drone's motion and the world, never from a roll of dice about the outcome.

## What Changes
- **The tether table** (physics): `assets-src/tether.json`, every number of the fiber, the spool, the link and the wires' end fittings, each labelled; its hash in the header.
- **One cable solver** (`Runtime/Tether/`): a lumped-mass chain stepped by linearly implicit Euler in the 1 kHz step (a block-tridiagonal solve, + − × ÷ only), so the fiber's and the conductors' axial stiffness never limits the step; tension-only segments; sliding contact nodes with the capstan law; nodes inserted and removed by fixed state rules.
- **The spool and the span** (PHY-18, DRN-05): one-way payout through the rear cap's exit when the tension at the exit passes the pack's peel resistance, rate-dependent and named (`payout_tension_n`); the line's momentum flux; the exit lip's capstan and bend; the coil's mass and inertia rebuilt as line leaves; ≤ 32 active nodes from the exit to where the line comes to rest, the rest a laid trail that lifts again when pulled; gravity, drag by Reynolds number, the wind field, the rotors' inflow and wash, and in rain a wet film.
- **The fiber's contacts:** the ground and its cover, shapes (skipping the homogenised `twig`, `foliage` and `razor_wire` shells), p7a's elements and hooks, snag records (barbs as lock points), the wires, the drone's own parts and the stand; a node on two members takes both (the wedge).
- **Cut risk, never certainty** (PHY-19): the line breaks by tension against the span's strength, by local stress (tension plus the elastica's bend over an edge, the exit lip or the peel point) against the strength of that fiber section, by abrasion worn through a section's coating, or by entering a turning prop disc. Strengths are named, bounded draws (one per flight for the span, one per 0.1 m section for local stress), each logged when first read.
- **The link margin** (PHY-20): the optical budget less attenuation, macrobend loss at every bend (fitted through ITU-T G.657's published limits) and microbend loss at loaded contacts; a dip is the margin crossing the receiver's threshold, logged with its cause.
- **The break** (PHY-21, PHY-25): the feed plays the staged loss, the flight controller's Betaflight 4.5 failsafe stages run and disarm; the link signals become live.
- **Soft wires** (PHY-22, D-059): every conductor swings in the wind as its first mode; near the drone or the fiber it becomes a chain with its record's mass, EA and tension, so it deflects with the published taut-string and Irvine stiffness, stretches, slides along a part with the `cable` friction, wraps an arm, a leg or a prop hub (a caught blade stalls its rotor or winds the wire), entangles, twists and flips the drone, and a hard enough strike breaks it at its breaking force or pulls an end out of its tie and drops it. Blade strikes go to `p7f-damage`'s per-blade slot.
- **The acceptance harness** (`flightbench --tether`, `--wires`): each of the pilot's accounts over many seeds, each target failing a known-wrong model.
- **The budget** (D-054): the fiber's and the wires' query batch, judged on physics' pattern inside PLT-04.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `fiber-tether`: the tether table, the cable solver, the spool and payout, the span and its laid trail, its loads, its contacts and snags, its pull on the drone, the coil's mass, cut risk and its strengths, abrasion, the link margin, the break and the failsafe, the signals to the feed, its randomness, determinism, budget and log, and the acceptance harness
- `wire-strikes`: soft conductors: the swing, activation into a chain, the published stiffness, contact with the drone's parts, sliding, wrapping, prop strikes and winding, entanglement and flips, breaking and dropping, their randomness, budget and log, and the acceptance harness

### Modified Capabilities
- `asset-pipeline`: the `wires` record gains `bending_stiffness_n_m2` (EI).
- `world-query` (still in flight in `p6-world-query-gallery`): ADDED requirements only (wires near a point with their ends and neighbours, wires left to physics, snag records near a point); when both changes archive, the orchestrator checks no field list goes stale.
- `analog-feed` (still in flight in `p4-feed`): ADDED requirement only (the link loss from the physics).

## Non-goals
- **New wire assets or damaged spans built by the generator** (L04, L05, `damage` cut and down): the range's five `L03_medium` spans are the release's; thin and thick spans run on synthetic worlds from `L03_thin`'s and `L03_thick`'s records. A wire the drone breaks or drops in flight is this change's.
- **Concertina wire on the map:** F01 is not built; barbs run on synthetic records with the fortifications reading's values.
- **Blade and motor damage** (`p7f-damage`): this change writes each wire strike to `BladeStrikes`; chipping, bending and breaking a blade are 7f's.
- **Turbulence funnelled through openings** (PHY-23's third clause): `p7c-wind`'s jets through gaps. Openings' edges and rebar already collide (1.6, 1.6b); this change adds the fiber's bend over them.
- **The feed's own free-running dropouts** (D-026, the pilot's chosen look) stay as the feed has them; physics adds the dips it causes (open question 1).
- **Fiber on water** (D-039) and a second drone's line: none on the range.
- **Ground and plant mechanics:** `p7a-ground`'s; the fiber reads its elements, hooks, soil lip and stand.
