  - **As built** (`Runtime/Tether/CableChain.cs`; not in the flight step yet, so no golden moves; `LogFormat` unchanged at 14, 18 since the merge onto the physics line, 1.1's note):
    - **The step:** `CableNode`s (position, velocity, material s, mass μ·(half of each neighbouring rest length), the caller's force, linearised drag and air, an anchor, two `CableContact` slots) joined by tension-only segments; `(M + dt·C + dt²·K)·v₁ = M·v₀ + dt·f₀` by Thomas over symmetric 3 × 3 blocks (cofactor inverses), positions from v₁. Every force enters as a quadratic model equal to its true energy at the step's start, chosen above it where the truth is not quadratic (design 2's as-built bullet), so backward Euler cannot gain energy on the models. Two guards keep that true of the real forces: an **active-set correction** (a touch or tightening the explicit prediction missed is made speculative and the step solved again from its start, at most twice) and the **energy check** (the models' exact deficit at the end: a rise within it is clipped from the motion about the mean velocity; a rise the motion cannot give back solves the step once more on majorizing models, `SafeSteps`; a rise after that, or a non-finite value, is a `CableWedge`, the step undone and the line stopped, for 1.6 to log as `TetherWedge`).
