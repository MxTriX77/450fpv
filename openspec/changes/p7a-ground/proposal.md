## Why
The pilot flew the map build and asked for "stucking, wobbling" (D-056), then: "straws and leaves still dont affect flight physics. in real life a straw or two even tho they are tiny may affect the flight - add wobbling, shaking, props getting stuck a bit etc." (D-059). Today the ground is rigid with one friction pair for every surface (`p6-flight-core` 1.6: "per-surface μ is 7a's"), the straw is the only give, and the test range is flat. The manifesto's first two complaints are about the ground: a heavy drone lifting off and a leg that is "randomly stuck for a moment", and a landing on "pitfalls, loose or porous soil, subtle grass which causes jitter". The pilot's account is the target: legs stick "sometimes, a little, more on soft ground, most of the time in rain". None of it may be scripted or drawn: it comes from the soil, the straw, the stems and the weather, so "the ground is not random, it is unknown, and the pilot learns it".

## What Changes
- **The surfaces table** (physics): every ground surface's mechanics, dry, wet and saturated, labelled, in a physics-owned file beside `physics_materials.json`; the materials gain an area law, a stroke limit and an elastic modulus for slender members.
- **Soil under the skids** (PHY-01): the elasto-plastic Winkler soil on each half-skid's line contact, in series with the leg at equal force, a stick-point friction anchor whose limit grows with ploughing, footprints that a re-landing finds; dispatched on `Surface != 0`, the plane and every soil shape alike.
- **Uneven ground** (PHY-04): the test range gains soil zones (a firm dirt track, a tilled field with furrows, a shelled patch of loose spoil with craters and pitfalls) from one ground function the floor is drawn from; S04's patches gain pits hidden under the draped straw; E01's buried filler faces never act (S16); `terrain_deep` and `bottomless` are logged as world bugs.
- **Compliant layers:** area-type rows scale by the touching part's contact size (about 3× on straw for a skid against the reference foot), every compliant row has a stroke limit past which the layer is solid and carries the part onto what lies beneath, and `bark` and `deadwood` limbs bend as cantilevers from their root.
- **Wet ground** (D-021, PHY-01): in Rain and Severe rain each surface takes its wet or saturated row: lower bearing, more set, adhesion and suction on unloading, wet straw that clings.
- **Vegetation acts on the drone** (PHY-03, PHY-05, D-059): S04's drawn stems, straws and twigs and V01's and V13's drawn twigs and leaves become physics elements, the very ones drawn. On the body and legs: standing stems and crown twigs push as cantilevers (touchdown jitter, drag and shake in weeds, straw and crowns), twigs roll under a skid, and an element that lies over a skid at first touch can hook it and pull back until its release force, keyed by its `Id`.
- **Props through straw, leaves and twigs** (PHY-05, X-6, D-059): an element entering a prop disc is struck at the blade's pass and costs the rotor its cut energy plus the mass the blade flings; the rotor takes a torque spike and a short rpm dip that the motor recovers, and a thick twig or a dense clump stalls it for a moment until it is cut. Each strike is logged. **The seam with `p7f-damage`:** this change applies the strike's mechanics and writes each strike to a per-blade slot that 7f reads in the same step to damage the blade; this change never damages a blade.
- **The launch stand as a free body** (PHY-06, D-022, W8): it rests on the soil, an arm can catch or slide on a post, and it tips or drags when caught.
- **The acceptance harness:** liftoffs and landings from many spots of each ground, dry and wet, graded against the pilot's account, each target failing a known-wrong model; the landing bounce and the glide landing on real ground; the landing's wobble.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `ground-contact`: the surfaces table, the soil under a line contact, friction, ploughing and footprints, uneven ground, the merge of an asset's soil shapes, compliant layers, wet ground, vegetation on the body and legs, hooks, prop strikes on vegetation and the damage seam, the free launch stand, liftoff stick and shake, the landing bounce and wobble, and the randomness, determinism, budget and log they keep

### Modified Capabilities
- `asset-pipeline`: the metadata contract gains micro elements, ground features and cantilever records; material rows gain their area law, stroke limit, elastic modulus and wet block; a micro mesh's physics elements are the drawn ones.
- `assets-earthworks-and-surfaces`: S04 carries its stems, straws and twigs as physics elements and its hidden pits.
- `assets-vegetation`: ADDED, V01's and V13's crowns carry their drawn twigs and leaves as physics elements and their limbs' cantilever records.
- `world-query` (still in flight in `p6-world-query-gallery`): ADDED requirements only (the test range's uneven ground, `MicroDetailNear`, a runtime object that moves, the broad phase inside each object); when both changes archive, the orchestrator checks no field list goes stale.

## Non-goals
- **New ground looks.** The zones use the existing ground materials as stand-ins (D-040); a look pass is its own change.
- **Rubble, leaf litter, char stubble, debris chunks:** no such ground is on the test range (D-047). Their rows are in the table; their contact cases run on synthetic worlds only.
- **The fiber on the ground** (7d): it will read this change's elements, hooks, soil edge radius and stand body.
- **Prop and motor damage** (`p7f-damage`, PHY-26): this change applies and logs each strike and fills the per-blade strike slot; chipping, bending and breaking a blade, and strikes on walls and trunks, are 7f's.
- **D-059's other parts:** soft, elastic wires that wrap and flip the drone (`p7d-tether`), and the camera that feels every movement (`p7e-camera-feel`).
- **The Godot stand-in trees' crowns** are in only if the orchestrator rules that D-040 allows their port physics records (task 1.8b); otherwise they wait for their Unity replacements.
- **A shape's own `stiffness_n_per_m` override** (S5): no asset on the test range needs it (R01's bumpers are chrome steel; F01 and plastic bumper covers are not on the map). It lands with the first asset that does.
- **E01's F-3 restated:** the contact law's buried-face rule makes the 25 mm rings harmless, so E01 is not rebuilt.
