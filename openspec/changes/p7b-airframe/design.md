# Design: p7b-airframe

## Context
- `p6-flight-core` has the Betaflight controller with the builder's filter chain (D-045), the DC motors on APC's curves with the 40 A ESC limit, two packs with sag, drag, the stand-in wind and contacts. Every rotor is identical and its inflow sees only the wind.
- Randomness rule: only a bounded perturbation of a named physical parameter, from a named seeded stream, logged; never noise on the pose.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** each PHY-07…12 mechanism physical, seeded where random, logged, with a computed band and a known-wrong model that fails it; determinism and the budget kept.
**Non-Goals:** the proposal's non-goals.

## Decisions
1. **Asymmetry from tolerances** (PHY-07): per motor Kv spread, ESC timing jitter and prop imbalance, each drawn once per flight within published or researched tolerances (labelled), named streams. The band of drift and correction rate with wind off is computed from them; with asymmetry off the drone is measurably steadier; it never buzzes or diverges.
   - **As built (1.1):** `FlightSetup.Asymmetry` (`MotorAsymmetry`, from `DroneSpec.BuildAsymmetry()`; null is off, for Calm's grading). Three streams, 16 draws at the flight's start, in the header with their bounds:
     - `motor.kv`: Kv × (1 + δ), δ nearly normal, σ 2 % (assumed: no lot spread is published), clamped to ±10 % (published: maxon's constants hold to about ±10 %). Ke = Kt moves with it.
     - `esc.lag`: τ_esc + Δ, Δ uniform in ±0.08 ms (derived: the winding's L/R at ±10 %, ±30 µs, and AM32's 20 kHz loop and 24 kHz PWM phase against the frame, ±46 µs, which drifts update by update below the 250 µs sub-step and is carried as one offset per flight, assumed).
     - `prop.unbalance`: U uniform in [0, U_per] at a uniform angle, U_per = m·G/Ω = 0.107 g·mm (G 6.3, published, Tyto Robotics; the 16 g prop; Ω at the props' 9000 rpm, assumed). Each rotor pulls its hub with U·ω² at its angle (`FlightState.RotorAngle`, logged).
   - **The band** is the linear steady state at the hover (`AsymmetryScenario.Predict`, 20 000 seeds' draws, 0.5–99.5 %): roll 1.5e-4–6.8e-2 °/s, yaw 1.2e-4–4.5e-2 °/s over a 30 s hands-off hover (yanks of 0.005–2.0° and 0.003–1.4°), the vibration 8.9e-5–4.2e-4 rad/s per step. The ESC lag and the unbalance make no steady moment: the Kv spread is the yank, the unbalance the never-still vibration.
2. **The per-rotor inflow seam** is shared with `p7c-wind`: one function gives each rotor its inflow (wind, own and neighbours' wash, ground and ceiling terms). Both changes write into it; this change defines it first.
   - **As built (1.1):** `Propulsion.RotorInflow(in FlightState s, Double3[] windAtHubs)`, called by `BeginStep` before the rotors' loads. Its outside input is the wind at each hub, world m/s in `Corners` order (null for still air); 7c's field plugs in where `FlightCore.Propel` fills `_hubWind`. It writes `Propulsion.Inflow` (axial, m/s, positive down through the disc) and `InPlane(i)` (body m/s). The own wash and VRS (1.2), the neighbours' wash and the ground and ceiling (1.3) are added inside it, from the state and the rotors' loads. The refactor left every golden bit for bit.
3. **Ground and ceiling effect** (PHY-08): Cheeseman–Bennett per rotor from a ray straight down the rotor axis, combined with `SampleGround`'s relief and mat top; a `Hole` switches the ground term off. The same form under a roof for the ceiling effect. Recirculation in a close hover: a bounded, physical re-ingestion of the own wash that keeps a close hover never still.
   - **As built (1.3, on the stub):** `FlightSetup.GroundEffect` (`GroundEffectSpec`, from `DroneSpec.BuildGroundEffect()`; null is off), per rotor inside `Propulsion.RotorInflow`, from the distances `RotorSurfaces` finds (FlightCore: two batched queries a step, 8 rays and 4 ground samples):
     - **The surfaces:** a ray down each rotor's axis within 8 R (1.02 m; derived: Cheeseman–Bennett's gain is 0.1 % there) and `SampleGround` at its hub; below is the nearer of the ray's hit and the sampled ground's plane along the axis (the ray sees the terrain's triangles, the sample its relief and mat top); a `Hole` drops the ground's sample and the ray never sees the ground there, while shapes still count. Above: a ray up the axis.
     - **The ground** (published: Cheeseman and Bennett 1955, ARC R&M 3021, its hover and forward-flight forms): T/T∞ = 1/(1 − (R/4z)²·v_h²/(v_h² + V²)), V the hub's in-plane airspeed, v_h at the rotor's own thrust for their v_i (assumed); less its value at the reach, so no step (−0.12 % at 0.3 D); held below 0.5 R (assumed; 1.333 there). Their ratio is at constant power; the torque at a speed is kept, as the ring keeps it, so at a fixed speed the power is fixed and the ratio is the thrust's gain there (derived).
     - **The ceiling** (published: Hsiao and Chirarattananon, IEEE/ASME T-Mech 2019, arXiv:1905.04632): γ = ½ + ½·√(1 + α₀δ²/8), δ = R/z, the thrust γ^(2/3) at constant power, α₀ 1.60 from their props at fixed drive (on a 10-inch prop, assumed; the forward fade, assumed); less its value at the reach. Not "the same form": the image-form ceiling fit of Sanchez-Cuevas et al. (ICUAS 2017) could not be checked against its text, so the measured momentum model stands in.
     - **Recirculation:** each disc re-ingests a zero-mean inflow x·v_h (its mean is inside the measured ground effect; the fountain's upwash and the re-ingested downwash), a first-order process, τ = 2(z + R)/v_h (assumed: the wash's loop), σ 0.05 v_h at 0.5 R falling linearly to 0 at 4 R (assumed: about 3 % of the thrust), bounded ±0.15 v_h (assumed, 3σ), its unit from the named stream `ground.recirc` (counter-based on the step and the corner), into the thrust's advance ratio as the ring's inflow. Never on the pose.
     - **Logged:** `StepTrace.GroundDistance` and `CeilingDistance`, `FlightState.WashInflow` in the step record (`LogFormat.Version` 8), the spec in the header.
     - **Not yet:** Sanchez-Cuevas et al.'s multirotor correction (the neighbours' images and the body's lift), and the test range's rays, holes and mats (`p6-world-query-gallery` 1.4).
3c. **The rotors' edgewise-flow moments** (PHY-17a's gust response, from `p7c-wind` 1.5's grading): each rotor's blades flap in the edgewise flow and load the hub, per rotor inside the seam (`Propulsion.HubMoment`).
   - **As built (1.3c):** `FlightSetup.Flapping` (`RotorFlappingSpec`, from `DroneSpec.BuildFlapping()`; null is off).
     - **The blade** (published: Johnson, *Helicopter Theory*, 1980, §5.2–5.4): linear lift on the sheet's chord (26 mm at 0.3 R to 8 mm at the tip; 18 mm at 0.1 R, assumed), the 4.5-inch helix less α_L0 −6.3° (derived: p7c 1.6's fit to APC's static thrust and torque), a = 5.7/rad (assumed, as p7c 1.6); the quasi-steady 0/rev and 1/rev flap on a spring-restrained centre hinge.
     - **The stiffness:** K_β = I_b·(ω₀² + (K_s − 1)·Ω²), I_b 4.3e-5 kg·m² a blade (derived: the 16 g prop as the mass build's uniform rod), ω₀ 150 Hz (assumed: a glass-filled nylon cantilever, 100–200 Hz), K_s 1.193 (published: a uniform cantilever's first mode, Bramwell 1976): ν 1.48 and the Lock number 0.55 (8·½ρa·R⁴·I₃/I_b) at 9000 rpm, so the prop is near the rigid limit (the flap 0.1–0.6°).
     - **The inflow:** Glauert's v_i at the rotor's thrust (momentum theory, published), and Pitt and Peters' skew κ_x = (15π/32)·tan(χ/2) (Vertica 1981, published), which makes the back flapping of a stiff prop.
     - **The moment:** (N_b/2)·K_β·(s·β₁s·X − β₁c·Y) over a turn. The rolling part (the dissymmetry of lift) changes sign with the spin and cancels across the quad's pairs; the pitching part (the discs tilting away from the airflow) adds over the four: 0.37 N·m at μ 0.05 (5.9 m/s), 0.56 at 0.10, 0.57 at 0.20. The 2/rev part is left out (assumed: 300 Hz).
     - **The H-force is kept as the only in-plane force** (at the hub, `props.h_force_n_per_m_s`): the flapping adds no tilted-thrust force T·β₁c, which is inside the H-force in shaft axes (Johnson), so nothing counts twice. On the stiff prop T·β₁c is 0.024–0.037 N per rotor at 5.9–11.7 m/s: 9–14 % of p7c 1.6's rigid-rotor H-force (0.044 N per m/s), 2 % of this branch's 0.225.
     - **Logged:** the spec in the header (`LogFormat.Version` 9, with the steady wind's onset for a gust step); the moment in `StepTrace.AeroMoment`.
3d. **The rotors in each other's wake** (D-060, PHY-13a, PHY-14's pitch targets, from `p7c-wind` 1.5's model-19 grade: pitch at 1–4 Hz 3× low against clip P): in forward flight each rotor's wake is swept back under the rotors behind it, whose discs take its downwash, and the rotors beside and ahead its upwash. Per rotor inside the seam (`Propulsion.RotorInflow`), as an axial inflow at the disc, as the wind's axial part enters it, so the thrust, the torque, the flapping and the ring all take it.
   - **As built (1.3d):** `FlightSetup.Interference` (`RotorInterferenceSpec`, `Runtime/Flight/RotorInterference.cs`, from `DroneSpec.BuildInterference()`, named `RotorWakeSpec` on its branch; null is the known-wrong "no interaction"):
     - **The wake** (published: Coleman, Feingold and Stempin 1945, NACA ARR L5E10; Castles and De Leeuw 1954, NACA Report 1184, the normal component of its induced velocity off the disc and a second rotor's interference from it): Coleman's uniform skewed vortex cylinder, rings of the disc's radius parallel to it, uniform in depth, swept downstream at the skew χ, tan χ = μ/λ, λ = V_a + v_i with Glauert's v_i at the rotor's thrust (momentum theory, as the flapping's). A ring's normal velocity in the disc's plane is the circular vortex's closed form (K and E by the arithmetic-geometric mean, Abramowitz and Stegun 17.6), integrated over the depth (nodes 1.08 apart from 1e-4 to 400 R, derived: 0.1 % of the fine sum).
     - **Its checks:** over the disc, Coleman's gradient tan(χ/2) within 2e-4 at χ 30, 60 and 75° and flat across it; in the plane outside an unskewed wake (a hover) nothing, the semi-infinite cylinder's end plane (exact; within 2e-3 v_i numerically, held at 0 in the table).
     - **The interference:** target j's added inflow Σ_i k_ij·v_i over the other rotors, k the target disc's average of the source's wake at its skew and the target's place in its flow, weighted along the blade by c·x per unit radius, as a blade's thrust takes an inflow (blade-element theory; 4 Gauss points on the sheet's chord × 12 azimuths). Tabled per distance between hubs (2.517 R beside and behind, 3.559 R diagonal) over tan(χ/2) by 0.025 to 0.9 (χ 84°, held there) and sin(φ/2) by 0.025, φ the target's angle off the source's downstream direction (assumed: the numerics; within 0.1 % of the exact average), built once per layout in 1.3 s and shared. One hub behind: 0.044 v_i at χ 20°, 0.135 at 40°, 0.350 at 60°, 0.578 at 70°, 1.01 at 80°; beside an upwash of 0.01–0.10 v_i, ahead 0.02–0.05, diagonally behind +0.01 to −0.14 (derived). The flight-dynamics form k·v_i, k from the skew and the place, rising to 2 in a flat wake (published: Chen, Yuan and Thomson 2021, Progress in Aerospace Sciences 120, §4).
     - **Formed only while the flow carries it away:** each source's wake fades from a transport velocity √(μ² + λ²) of v_h (assumed: a hover's own, so hover, climb and forward flight always have it whole) to Newman et al.'s 0.74 v_h (published, as the ring's design takes it), below which the wake piles up round its disc and the vortex ring owns it. A reversed flow through the disc (λ < 0) takes the mirrored skew (a ring's normal field is even about its plane).
     - **Its own unsteadiness:** each rotor's interference × (1 + x), x a first-order process with the corner of R-sized eddies carried past at the rotor's in-plane airspeed (24 Hz at the cruise; assumed), σ 0.05 and ±0.15 (assumed: the tip vortices pass 0.04–0.12 m under the rear discs' leading half at the cruise and wander by 1–2 % of R in a young model-rotor wake, recalled; no measured fluctuation of a rotor in another's wake below the blade pass was found), its unit from the named stream `rotor.interference` (`wake.rotor` on its branch), counter-based on the step and the corner. Never on the pose. The tip vortices' passage at 2/rev (290–350 Hz) is vibration (hover-micro-motion.md §4) and left out.
     - **Left out (assumed):** the wake's downward bend as its inflow doubles (Heyson and Katzoff), which moves it away from the rotors behind; the wake's in-plane velocity at the target; a second pass of the interference into the sources' own skews.
     - **What it gives** (still air, the harness pilot holding a pitch, the last 10 s; `RotorInterferenceScenario`; a hover held by SimPilot takes exactly none): the rear pair's thrust at its own speed under the front pair's by 1.7 % at 2.9 m/s (rear +0.8 %, front −0.9 %), 5.5 % at 10 m/s, 6.5 % at 15 m/s and 6.2 % at clip P's cruise (20.6 m/s at 22.7°: v_i 4.25 m/s, χ 57°, the rear rotors' added inflow 0.91 m/s, the front's −0.59 m/s; the rear rotors 3.7 % short of an isolated rotor at their speed, the front 2.6 % over it). The loop spins the rear rotors 1.6 % faster to hold the pitch; the cruise's airspeed is unchanged (20.57 m/s). On other frames the published losses are 11 % (Ye, Wang and Lv 2021, Aerospace 8(11) 349, CFD of an X quadcopter at 5–15 m/s, negligible in a hover and at 25 m/s), 19 % (Misiorowski, Gandhi and Oberai 2019, AIAA Journal 57(12), DES at 10 m/s and 5° nose down, the loss at the aft rotors' front; the plus configuration's side rotors gain 5.5–7.6 % from upwash) and at most 11 % rear under front (Nguyen, Liu and Mori 2018, Trans. JSASS 61(1), a tandem wind-tunnel test that a Biot–Savart formula reproduced qualitatively). The uniform cylinder gives about half of them (no roll-up or contraction of the wake); its sign, its rise with the skew, its upwash beside and its absence in a hover are theirs.
     - **Logged:** `StepTrace.InterferenceInflow` and `FlightState.InterferenceShare` in the step record after model 19's 1808 bytes (`LogFormat.Version` 15), the spec in the header (tag 22), the stream in the randomness entry.
3e. **The drone is never perfectly still** (D-064, PHY-24): three sources, each a bounded perturbation of a named parameter from a named stream, logged, never on the pose; graded against the pilot's footage (`docs/reference-notes/ground-micro-motion.md` §6) by `GroundMicroMotionProbe`, with a perfectly steady rotor as the known-wrong.
   - **As built (1.10):**
     - **Each rotor's own wake** (`RotorWakeSpec`, `FlightSetup.RotorWake`; null is the steady rotor): its wake inflow through the disc, x·v_h, a first-order process from `rotor.wake` (counter-based on the step and the corner), into the thrust's and the torque's advance ratio. Thrust σ 2.9 % in hover (derived from published: Stack, Caradonna and Savaş 2004, a 25.4 cm rotor in hover, 12 % peak to peak below the rev over 90 revolutions, read as 4.2σ), bounded above by Zhou, Ning and Hu 2021 (7 % over 0–1500 Hz, vibration included); its 2.4× neighbour gain at a 0.05 D tip gap is not applied (the ratio of the whole σ, tied to the blade-passage loading, not the wake's wander). Converted to inflow through the props' static slope, σ_x = σ_T·(1 − r)/(2r) (derived; 1.5 at r 0.25). Corner f·R/v_h 0.2 (published for the vortex ring's low band, Johnson 2005, Table 2; for the hover's wake assumed): 15 Hz at the hover's v_h. Faded in forward flight by Glauert's v_i/v_h (published). Bound 3σ (assumed). The four rotors independent (assumed).
     - **The re-ingested wash on published numbers** (`GroundEffectSpec`): the onset at H/R 4 and the loop T_conv ≈ 0.1 s at H/R 2 (Dekker et al. 2023, Flow 3 E30: the fountain re-ingested at H/R 2, reaching 3 R at H/R 4) give RecirculationLoop 3.0 (derived, 9.1 R/v_h over 3 R); the level 3.3 % of thrust at H/R 2 (Healy et al. 2021's "up to 10 %" read as 3σ, assumed), linear to 0 at 4 R, held below 0.5 R; bound 0.17 (3σ there). The wash and the wake now move the torque through the props' published torque against J (the ring keeps the freestream's, as measured).
     - **Each motor's own dynamics** (`MotorAsymmetry`): the winding resistance (±10 %, published: E-JPC VH's line-to-line tolerance), the no-load current (±10 %, published: maxon's test specification), σ a third of each (assumed); the rotor inertia ±3 %, σ 1 % (assumed); streams `motor.resistance`, `motor.friction`, `motor.inertia`. With Kv they set each motor's τ_m, trim and idle.
     - **Logged:** `FlightState.WakeInflow` at the record's end, after the interference (`LogFormat.Version` 15, 1904 bytes), the wake's spec (tag 23), the asymmetry's new tolerances and the recirculation's reference height in the header.
3f. **The own wake's unsteadiness away from the hover** (D-064's first source in 3e; the physics ledger's gaps 3 and 10; `p7c-wind` design 11, D-105). 3e sized the wake's wander on the hover (σ_T 2.9 %, Stack et al. 2004) and faded it in forward flight only to Glauert's v_i/v_h (0.49 at the cruise, μ 2), with its corner at the hover's v_h whatever the flow. So at clip P's cruise, where the wake is swept a disc diameter behind the rotor in 13 ms, half the hover's wander stayed: 0.19° of Severe's 0.23° pitch, flown by the fixed-vector wind as well.
   - **The mechanism** (labelled): the wander a disc feels is induced at it by its own near wake, a sheet of vorticity of strength γ = 2·v_i (a vortex cylinder's, published: momentum theory and Coleman's cylinder, as 3d), whose filaments wander further the older they are. The tip vortices' positional scatter grows with wake age (published: Leishman, Exp. Fluids 25, 1998, one- and two-bladed hovering rotors; Karpatne, Sirohi et al., Exp. Fluids 54, 2013); its growth taken linear in age (assumed). The wake leaves the disc at its transport velocity V_t = √(V_∥² + (V_a + v_i)²) (Glauert's resultant through the disc, published), so a filament s off the disc is s/V_t old and wanders δ ∝ s/V_t; the inflow it moves at the disc goes as γ·δ/s², so the disc's inflow σ ∝ ∫ v_i·(s/V_t)/s² ds ∝ v_i/V_t over the same near-wake depth (derived; the depth's logarithm is the hover's and cancels in the ratio). Momentum theory gives v_i·V_t = v_h² (Glauert, published), so **the inflow's σ over v_h is the hover's times (v_i/v_h)²**, and the wake's disturbances pass the disc at V_t, so **the corner is CornerFrequency·V_t/R** (derived: the residence time R/V_t; the hover's 0.2·v_h/R kept, assumed as in 3e). A measured thrust fluctuation of a small rotor against its advance ratio below the rev was searched for and not found (the stands that measure edgewise flow report means); the law is the derivation's, every input published but the linear growth.
   - **Each flight state** (the axial inflow a = V_a/v_h positive as in a climb, μ = V_∥/v_h; v = v_i/v_h from Glauert's v·√(μ² + (a + v)²) = 1):
     - **Hover:** v 1, σ and corner the hover's (3e's, unchanged).
     - **Forward flight:** the wake swept back. At μ 0.5 v 0.94 (σ ×0.88, corner ×1.06); μ 1, 0.79 (×0.62, ×1.27); clip P's cruise μ ≈ 2, 0.49 (**×0.236**, ×2.06: 32 Hz). The old law's ×0.49 at the cruise was the first power.
     - **A climb:** the wake leaves faster: a 0.3, v 0.86 (×0.74, ×1.16); a 1, 0.62 (×0.38).
     - **A descent:** the wake comes back toward the disc and lingers: by momentum theory v rises to 1.105 at a −0.2 (×1.22, corner ×0.905). Momentum theory holds only to Johnson's VzD (η 0.2, published, NASA/TP-2005-213477 Table 4: there his measured inflow leaves it), so the law is held there, `RotorWakeSpec.DescentHold` 0.2 (a const, published; `VortexRingSpec.ExitRate` is the same point). Deeper is the vortex ring's (design 4): its σ is 0 at VzD and rises to 0.10 at VzX, so it is the ring's own part, added in quadrature to this held one; at the ring's peak the sum is √(0.035² + 0.10²) = 0.106, within Shetty and Selig's ±30 %-read-as-3σ (published). Nothing counts twice: below VzD there is no ring, above it the wake is not raised further.
     - **Near the ground:** the wake's return off the ground is the re-ingested wash's (3e's `GroundEffectSpec`, onset 4 R, Dekker et al. 2023); this term takes the hub's own air only, so the close hover keeps the hover's wake and the wash adds its own.
   - **The bound:** Bound (3σ) times the state's σ (3e: the hover's whatever the state).
   - **What it should change:** the cruise's own-wake pitch at 1–4 Hz by about σ·√τ: 0.236 × √0.486 against 0.49 → 0.34 of the old (0.19° → about 0.065°); the hover and the close hover nothing (μ and a near 0); the punch's descents the held 1.22× (but see the shake below).
