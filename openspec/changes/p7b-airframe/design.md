# Design: p7b-airframe

## Context
- `p6-flight-core` has the Betaflight controller with the builder's filter chain (D-045), the DC motors on APC's curves with the 40 A ESC limit, two packs with sag, drag, the stand-in wind and contacts. Every rotor is identical and its inflow sees only the wind.
- Randomness rule: only a bounded perturbation of a named physical parameter, from a named seeded stream, logged; never noise on the pose.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** each PHY-07…12 mechanism physical, seeded where random, logged, with a computed band and a known-wrong model that fails it; determinism and the budget kept.
**Non-Goals:** the proposal's non-goals.

## Decisions
1. **Asymmetry from tolerances** (PHY-07): per motor Kv spread, ESC timing jitter and prop imbalance, each drawn once per flight within published or researched tolerances (labelled), named streams. The band of drift and correction rate with wind off is computed from them; with asymmetry off the drone is measurably steadier; it never buzzes or diverges.
   - **As built (1.1):** `FlightSetup.Asymmetry` (`MotorAsymmetry`, from `DroneSpec.BuildAsymmetry()`; null is off, for Calm's grading). Three streams, 16 draws at the flight's start, in the header with their bounds:
     - `motor.kv`: Kv × (1 + δ), δ nearly normal, σ 2 % (assumed: no lot spread is published), clamped to ±10 % (published: maxon's constants hold to about ±10 %). Ke = Kt moves with it.
     - `esc.lag`: τ_esc + Δ, Δ uniform in ±0.08 ms (derived: the winding's L/R at ±10 %, ±30 µs, and AM32's 20 kHz loop and 24 kHz PWM phase against the frame, ±46 µs, which drifts update by update below the 250 µs sub-step and is carried as one offset per flight, assumed).
     - `prop.unbalance`: U uniform in [0, U_per] at a uniform angle, U_per = m·G/Ω = 0.107 g·mm (G 6.3, published, Tyto Robotics; the 16 g prop; Ω at the props' 9000 rpm, assumed). Each rotor pulls its hub with U·ω² at its angle (`FlightState.RotorAngle`, logged).
   - **The band** is the linear steady state at the hover (`AsymmetryScenario.Predict`, 20 000 seeds' draws, 0.5–99.5 %): roll 1.5e-4–6.8e-2 °/s, yaw 1.2e-4–4.5e-2 °/s over a 30 s hands-off hover (yanks of 0.005–2.0° and 0.003–1.4°), the vibration 8.9e-5–4.2e-4 rad/s per step. The ESC lag and the unbalance make no steady moment: the Kv spread is the yank, the unbalance the never-still vibration.
2. **The per-rotor inflow seam** is shared with `p7c-wind`: one function gives each rotor its inflow (wind, own and neighbours' wash, ground and ceiling terms). Both changes write into it; this change defines it first.
   - **As built (1.1):** `Propulsion.RotorInflow(in FlightState s, Double3[] windAtHubs)`, called by `BeginStep` before the rotors' loads. Its outside input is the wind at each hub, world m/s in `Corners` order (null for still air); 7c's field plugs in where `FlightCore.Propel` fills `_hubWind`. It writes `Propulsion.Inflow` (axial, m/s, positive down through the disc) and `InPlane(i)` (body m/s). The own wash and VRS (1.2), the neighbours' wash and the ground and ceiling (1.3) are added inside it, from the state and the rotors' loads. The refactor left every golden bit for bit.
3. **Ground and ceiling effect** (PHY-08): Cheeseman–Bennett per rotor from a ray straight down the rotor axis, combined with `SampleGround`'s relief and mat top; a `Hole` switches the ground term off. The same form under a roof for the ceiling effect. Recirculation in a close hover: a bounded, physical re-ingestion of the own wash that keeps a close hover never still.
   - **As built (1.3, on the stub):** `FlightSetup.GroundEffect` (`GroundEffectSpec`, from `DroneSpec.BuildGroundEffect()`; null is off), per rotor inside `Propulsion.RotorInflow`, from the distances `RotorSurfaces` finds (FlightCore: two batched queries a step, 8 rays and 4 ground samples):
     - **The surfaces:** a ray down each rotor's axis within 8 R (1.02 m; derived: Cheeseman–Bennett's gain is 0.1 % there) and `SampleGround` at its hub; below is the nearer of the ray's hit and the sampled ground's plane along the axis (the ray sees the terrain's triangles, the sample its relief and mat top); a `Hole` drops the ground's sample and the ray never sees the ground there, while shapes still count. Above: a ray up the axis.
     - **The ground** (published: Cheeseman and Bennett 1955, ARC R&M 3021, its hover and forward-flight forms): T/T∞ = 1/(1 − (R/4z)²·v_h²/(v_h² + V²)), V the hub's in-plane airspeed, v_h at the rotor's own thrust for their v_i (assumed); less its value at the reach, so no step (−0.12 % at 0.3 D); held below 0.5 R (assumed; 1.333 there). Their ratio is at constant power; the torque at a speed is kept, as the ring keeps it, so at a fixed speed the power is fixed and the ratio is the thrust's gain there (derived).
     - **The ceiling** (published: Hsiao and Chirarattananon, IEEE/ASME T-Mech 2019, arXiv:1905.04632): γ = ½ + ½·√(1 + α₀δ²/8), δ = R/z, the thrust γ^(2/3) at constant power, α₀ 1.60 from their props at fixed drive (on a 10-inch prop, assumed; the forward fade, assumed); less its value at the reach. Not "the same form": the image-form ceiling fit of Sanchez-Cuevas et al. (ICUAS 2017) could not be checked against its text, so the measured momentum model stands in.
     - **Recirculation:** each disc re-ingests a zero-mean inflow x·v_h (its mean is inside the measured ground effect; the fountain's upwash and the re-ingested downwash), a first-order process, τ = 2(z + R)/v_h (assumed: the wash's loop), σ 0.05 v_h at 0.5 R falling linearly to 0 at 4 R (assumed: about 3 % of the thrust), bounded ±0.15 v_h (assumed, 3σ), its unit from the named stream `ground.recirc` (counter-based on the step and the corner), into the thrust's advance ratio as the ring's inflow. Never on the pose.
     - **Logged:** `StepTrace.GroundDistance` and `CeilingDistance`, `FlightState.WashInflow` in the step record (`LogFormat.Version` 8), the spec in the header.
     - **Not yet:** Sanchez-Cuevas et al.'s multirotor correction (the neighbours' images and the body's lift), and the test range's rays, holes and mats (`p6-world-query-gallery` 1.4).
3c. **The rotors' edgewise-flow moments** (PHY-17a's gust response, from `p7c-wind` 1.5's grading): each rotor's blades flap in the edgewise flow and load the hub, per rotor inside the seam (`Propulsion.HubMoment`).
   - **As built (1.3c):** `FlightSetup.Flapping` (`RotorFlappingSpec`, from `DroneSpec.BuildFlapping()`; null is off).
     - **The blade** (published: Johnson, *Helicopter Theory*, 1980, §5.2–5.4): linear lift on the sheet's chord (26 mm at 0.3 R to 8 mm at the tip; 18 mm at 0.1 R, assumed), the 4.5-inch helix less α_L0 −6.3° (derived: p7c 1.6's fit to APC's static thrust and torque), a = 5.7/rad (assumed, as p7c 1.6); the quasi-steady 0/rev and 1/rev flap on a spring-restrained centre hinge.
     - **The stiffness:** K_β = I_b·(ω₀² + (K_s − 1)·Ω²), I_b 4.3e-5 kg·m² a blade (derived: the 16 g prop as the mass build's uniform rod), ω₀ 150 Hz (assumed: a glass-filled nylon cantilever, 100–200 Hz), K_s 1.193 (published: a uniform cantilever's first mode, Bramwell 1976): ν 1.48 and the Lock number 0.55 (8·½ρa·R⁴·I₃/I_b) at 9000 rpm, so the prop is near the rigid limit (the flap 0.1–0.6°).
     - **The inflow:** Glauert's v_i at the rotor's thrust (momentum theory, published), and Pitt and Peters' skew κ_x = (15π/32)·tan(χ/2) (Vertica 1981, published), which makes the back flapping of a stiff prop.
     - **The moment:** (N_b/2)·K_β·(s·β₁s·X − β₁c·Y) over a turn. The rolling part (the dissymmetry of lift) changes sign with the spin and cancels across the quad's pairs; the pitching part (the discs tilting away from the airflow) adds over the four: 0.37 N·m at μ 0.05 (5.9 m/s), 0.56 at 0.10, 0.57 at 0.20. The 2/rev part is left out (assumed: 300 Hz).
     - **The H-force is kept as the only in-plane force** (at the hub, `props.h_force_n_per_m_s`): the flapping adds no tilted-thrust force T·β₁c, which is inside the H-force in shaft axes (Johnson), so nothing counts twice. On the stiff prop T·β₁c is 0.024–0.037 N per rotor at 5.9–11.7 m/s: 9–14 % of p7c 1.6's rigid-rotor H-force (0.044 N per m/s), 2 % of this branch's 0.225.
     - **Logged:** the spec in the header (`LogFormat.Version` 9, with the steady wind's onset for a gust step); the moment in `StepTrace.AeroMoment`.
4. **Vortex ring state** (PHY-09): onset from the descent rate normalised by the induced velocity at the current thrust, so thrust against weight drives it; hysteresis between entry and exit; thrust loss and an asymmetric, wandering moment while inside. Entry and exit are logged events.
   - **As built (1.2):** `FlightSetup.VortexRing` (`VortexRingSpec`, from `DroneSpec.BuildVortexRing()`; null is off), per rotor inside `Propulsion.RotorInflow`, its state moved once a step by `Propulsion.AdvanceRing`:
     - **The scale:** each rotor's v_h = √(T/2ρA) at its own thrust then (momentum theory); η = −V_a/v_h along its axis, μ = |V_∥|/v_h in its plane.
     - **In and out** (Johnson 2005, NASA/TP-2005-213477, Table 4, published): in at η ≥ 0.45 (VzN, the flight tests' stability boundary) with μ < 0.95 (VxM); out below η 0.2 (VzD, where his VRS inflow rejoins momentum theory), past η 2.0 (VzE, the windmill brake state) or at μ ≥ 0.95. Using D as the exit is assumed. Events `VortexRingEntered` and `VortexRingLeft` (12, 13) carry η, μ and v_h.
     - **The mean loss:** the ring's inflow through the disc is Johnson's measured mean, momentum theory at D, 0.85 v_h at N, 1.25 v_h at X (η 1.5), linear between (assumed), back to the baseline at E. It is taken over the props' held descent branch (J −0.05, η 0.19, inflow 0.909 v_h, derived), so it costs thrust from η 0.61 (derived; where Newman et al.'s axial onset, a wake transport velocity of 0.74 v_h matched to Drees and Hendal, published, falls on momentum theory: 0.61) to 2.0, at most 0.34 v_h at X. It enters the thrust table as an advance ratio, 2δ/((1 − r)nD), r = −s·√(2C_T/π) = 0.25 from the table's static slope (derived, linearised at hover); the torque keeps the freestream's (Washizu; Azuma and Obata: little torque change, published). Lagged by Johnson's τ = 0.7/λ_h revolutions = 4.4 R/v_h (published).
     - **The wobble:** each rotor's thrust × (1 + x), x a first-order process with the corner f·R/v_h = 0.2 (the low band of Johnson's Table 2, published) and σ rising from 0 at D to 0.10 at X and to 0 at E (Shetty and Selig 2011, AIAA 2011-1254: 10-inch APC props, ±30 % at ±3σ at the minimum thrust, published; linear between, assumed), bounded ±0.30; its unit draws from the named stream `vrs.thrust`, counter-based on the step and the corner, so the four rings wander apart. Never on the pose.
     - **Logged:** `FlightState.RingInflow`, `RingThrust`, `RingLatched` in the step record (`LogFormat.Version` 7), the spec in the header.
5. **The throttle-punch shake** (PHY-10): the arms' bending and the loads' strapped mounts as small physical modes about the rigid body (D-050: no pendulum), and the rigid body's own jitter and tilt while a sharp arrest of a fast descent holds an ESC on its current limit, the assumed 40 A policy (D-051). Its occurrence and duration (as long as the arrest, D-049, D-051: 0.3–0.7 s for most of the empty drone's moderate descents, longer as the payload and a faster descent lengthen the arrest) come out of descent rate, input abruptness and payload; the filter chain (D-045) shapes what reaches the loop. The modes feed `CameraVibration`.
   - **As built (1.4):** `FlightSetup.Modes` (`AirframeModesSpec`, from `DroneSpec.BuildModes()`; null is a rigid airframe), solved once by `AirframeModes`:
     - **The degrees of freedom:** each arm a cantilever from the plates' clamp to the motor mount, its tip bending along +y, k = 3EI/L³ (Euler–Bernoulli, published) of the 25 × 6 mm plate (published, the CX10 ECO class) over 0.18 m (derived), E 55 GPa (assumed inside woven 3K plate's published 45–70): 12.7 kN/m; the tip the motor, the prop and 33/140 of the arm (Rayleigh, published), 0.140 kg; the clamp rigid (assumed). The payload and the coil three ways each, on strapped mounts (D-050: the bottle strapped rigidly on top, the coil in its cradle beneath; no sling), 30 kN/m every way (assumed, 10–100), each translating with its own inertia turning with the frame (assumed: its rocking on the straps left out). Damping ζ 0.02 for the arms (assumed: the laminate's 0.3–1 % and the bolted clamp), 0.1 for a mount (assumed); classical damping. On D-050's layout at 1 kg: the bottle's mount 27.6–30.0 Hz, the coil's 34.1–36.2 Hz, the arms 48.0, 54.5, 61.2 and 63.4 Hz.
     - **The modes:** free-free about the rigid body (mean axes; Waszak and Schmidt 1988, published): the mass matrix with the rigid body free, M̃ = M − Bᵀ·M_rr⁻¹·B, against K, by Cholesky and cyclic Jacobi (basic operations and square roots), mass-normalised. The rigid body moves exactly as without them; each mode is driven by the rotors' step-mean thrust at the tips less the inertial relief of the body's whole load (gravity drives none); semi-implicit Euler, the damping implicit.
     - **The coupling:** the frame's elastic turn joins the body rate the gyro reads (the builder's notches act on the frame's own resonance) and `CameraAngularVelocity`; each hub's elastic velocity joins its rotor's inflow (`Propulsion.SetHubElastic`), so the prop damps its arm. The camera at drone.json's `camera.lens_m`, (0, 0.015, 0.115) m (assumed: where the drone model puts the lens; model 13's (0, 0.014, 0.1) is the default for a file without one).
     - **The camera's vibration** (`VibrationBands`): the mount's acceleration each step (the body origin's, α × r + ω × (ω × r), and the frame's elastic share), second-order Butterworth sections at 5 (assumed: below it the pose carries the flight's own motion), 50 and 200 Hz, RMS over the 20 ms field: `FeedSignals.CameraVibration` X, Y, Z. An output only.
     - **Logged:** `FlightState.ModeShape`, `ModeRate` (ten each, 0 past the body's count) in the step record (`LogFormat.Version` 11), the spec in the header (tag 20; format 12 drops the slings' fields). The modes' constant mass through a flight (the fiber's pay-out is 7d's) are assumed.
     - **What it shows** (tasks 1.4's findings, D-051; 1.8's table, `flightbench --shake`, 40 seeds a cell): the visible shake is mostly the rigid body's. Through a full punch's arrest an ESC sits on its current limit for the whole arrest and all four for its first part (the descending props' heavier load), so the loop is short of authority and the attitude jitters and tilts 2.7–10.0° uncommanded; with an ESC that senses no current the tilt falls below half on every seed (0.1–3.2°) and the arrest takes 0.5–0.7 of the time. The rings coincide with the arrest but are not its cause: with them off the tilt is 0.62–1.70 of it on the same seed. The modes carry the 50–200 Hz band.
6. **Battery and inertia checks** (PHY-11, 12, D-048): the heavy corner (6.5 kg) shown to lose its thrust margin with the sag and to have none left at the cut-off; a low-voltage signal for the OSD at 3.00 V a cell loaded (pilot), which sets at the knee; the inertia ratios as computed from the real `drone.json`'s layout, asserted exactly, and yaw saturating first.
7. **Labels and bands:** every new number pilot / measured / published / derived / assumed; every assumed one a named, tunable parameter; each mechanism's scenario fails its known-wrong model.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- **The shake can't be tuned to a number** (the pilot: it "doesn't happen much"). Its check is that it arises from its causes and is rare, not that it hits a rate.
- **Ground effect needs the world's rays over the test range** (`p6-world-query-gallery` 1.4). The stub floor carries the first scenarios.
- **Each mechanism moves the goldens.** One re-record per task, audited.
