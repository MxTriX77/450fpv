  **As built:** `Editor/TopDown/TopDownMaps.cs`, its params in `TopDownMaps.json` (per map: id, scene, `x_m`, `z_m`, `width_px`, `height_px`). Each scene is drawn orthographically from straight above, north up and east right, under its own light (the friend's sun and sky as the scene saves them; the range's LookSetup at its start preset, day), as a plan: no fog or post, every tree and LOD group at full detail, no terrain grass (it draws only near the camera, so it would show as a disc), rendered at 2× and averaged 2 × 2. `Fpv450.Build.Windows` renders both maps before it builds, into `unity/Generated/topdown/<map-id>.png` and `.json` (git-ignored), and carries all of `unity/Generated/` into `StreamingAssets/generated/`; a failed render fails the build, which now needs graphics. **Extents:** Village x and z [−250, 1250] m at 2048² (0.73 m a pixel): the 1 km map and the surround's first 250 m, where it still carries the map's ground on before its generic hills, so the map keeps two thirds of the image; the whole 3 km surround would leave the village a few pixels. House x and z [−256, 256] m at 2048² (0.25 m a pixel): the range's map, its placement file's `extent_m`. **The transform** (`<map-id>.json`, documented in the class): u = (x − x0) / (x1 − x0) · W from the left edge, v = (z1 − z) / (z1 − z0) · H from the top; a heading ψ points along (sin ψ, −cos ψ). **Checked:** `Fpv450.TopDownMaps.Check` (magenta markers found on the PNG against the transform): Village's start 0.29 px and ruin 1646 0.27 px (the marker's edges snap to the 2× render's pixels, ≤ 0.25 px an axis), House's track start 0.00 px and house 0000 0.01 px; the package's and the scene's positions agree exactly (one frame, D-019).   **As built:** `Scripts/UI/MapPanel.cs`: the panel, its own component (IMGUI, as the app's screens), and `MapTransform`, which reads 1.1's JSON and maps (x, z) to image pixels by its transform. The hooks are one line each: `FlightScene.Attach` adds it on the flight with the flight's map id (`Host.Inputs.WorldId`, read at the scene's start, never hard-coded), the drawn drone body as the target (its pose is the sim's interpolated PoseFrame, which the view already reads; no new read of the sim) and `Covered` as hidden; `FarmlandExplore.Open` adds it on the noclip camera with `FlightWorlds.FarmlandMap`. M (`MapPanel.ToggleKey`) is read in the panel's own Update, like R, C, X and V, none of KeyboardFallback's keys; nothing pauses. The image is decoded once at the scene's start, with mipmaps (2048², 12 mips, about 0.2 s, under the loading screen), from `unity/Generated/topdown/` in the editor and `StreamingAssets/generated/topdown/` in the player.
