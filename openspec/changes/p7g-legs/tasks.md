  **As built (model 37, D-072; the log format unchanged).**
  - **The material.** Rigid PVC-U tube, 10 × 8 mm, for the legs and the skids (one stock), in printed PETG clamps and T-joints. The pilot's "plastic, a little soft" sets the family, T the shape: a smooth Ø10 mm tube, which rigid PVC is sold as (10 mm OD × 8 mm ID). The standing check rules out the softer candidates: the four legs sway-buckle (fixed at the clamps, the feet pinned) at π²EI/(4L²) a leg, 3.78 times its share of the take-off weight in PVC-U (E 3.0 GPa); PA6 conditioned (1.2 GPa, BASF Ultramid B3S), PA12 and PP (1.1–1.5 GPa) stand at 1.5 or less. Extruded PETG (2.0 GPa, 2.5 times) is the near alternative.
  - **Sources** (every value labelled and noted in `drone.json`'s `legs.tube`, `legs.clamp`, `legs.tjoint`): Angst+Pfister APSOplast PVC-U data sheets 00.001-01, 00.001-10 and 00.005-01 (E 3000–3300 MPa, σ_y 55–58, bending strength 80, elongation at break 15–20 %, 1.44–1.45 g/cm³, notched Charpy 2–4 kJ/m²); Reichelt Chemietechnik's PVC-U pipe (σ_y 50, E 3000, 1.42); Prusament PETG data sheet v1.1 (printed: E 1.5 ± 0.1 GPa, interlayer 18 ± 4 MPa); Imrhan and Munoz-Vasa 2013 (a 19 mm flange pinched and tightened: 0.768 ± 0.114 N·m); DIN 315 (a 20 mm wing nut is M4); Shigley's Table 8-15 (K 0.20 zinc-plated, 0.16–0.30); the polymer pairs' friction (Engineering ToolBox, the repository's plastic row: 0.3, 0.2–0.4, assumed). Assumed and named: the clamp's edge factor K_t 1.5 (1.2–2.0; press-fit edges are charted by Strozzi et al. 2016 and Croccolo et al. 2012, no value for this joint was found), the cracked tube's stiffness factor 0.5 (design 3's), the T-joint's M3 pinch screw at 0.5 N·m (0.3–0.7).

  **As built (model 39, format 27, event kinds 23–27; D-072, D-074).**
  - **The foot's side give** (`ContactSpec.LegSideStiffness`, `ContactModel.LegTrials`/`LegSums`). Each leg is a spring along its tube (52.3 kN/m, in series with the ground's material, as before) and across it at the foot: 1.1's 262 N/m in series with the stick anchor (0.5 × the first), 260 N/m. A point's push into the leg, d = δ_max·n − the anchor's stretch, splits along and across the tube, so a gripping foot loads the clamp through the tube's bending, and a tilted leg pushes its foot along its tube (1.1's strut; the level leg is the brief's series rule exactly). Its damper across is the tube's own, ζ = η/2 = 0.015 from rigid PVC's published loss factor (`legs.tube.loss_factor` 0.03, new in drone.json, labelled; the jaws' and the T-joint's friction left out, a lower bound): with the legs' assumed ζ 0.2 carried across, the side damper alone cracked all four legs on the scripted pilot's normal 0.44 m/s landing (a temporary edit), at 0.015 they stay intact. The leg's force acts on the body at the real foot, the contact point less the anchor's stretch (the leg is massless between the clamp and the foot): on the body's own point the soft leg let the support wander centimetres, and a drone set down past its tip righted itself.
  - **The failure law** (`Runtime/Contact/LegDamage.cs`, beside the contact model since `p7f`'s damage code has not landed). Each step each leg's loads (its points' forces within their cones, its tube shapes', `LegLoads`), then in order: the clamp's friction circle (pull 2μP, twist μPD; the slip takes the spring's excess pull, past the 16.5 mm grip the leg is `Detached`; a twist turns the jaws when it is the larger share); buckling past 20.19·EI/L² (385 N), the bow taking the excess shortening, w = √(Δ·L/2.5816), its moment at the clamp 0.7151·P·w (the fixed–pinned mode's constants, checked by quadrature); the clamp edge's moment (the spring's force across × the 0.2335 m lever, the tubes', the bow's) against the section under its axial load, s = |P|/(σ_y·A): first yield at M_c = (1 − s)·σ_y·I/(c·K_t), where it cracks (design 3) and where the cracked edge yields from then on (with the cracked factor on M_y every crack would part the tube at 0.70 M_y, under the crack's own onset for K_t below 1.4, so no leg would ever keep the pilot's "bend"); the set by the tube's moment–curvature law in the rectangle's closed form at its own shape factor (1.403, f(1 + s) under load), the plastic curvature spread over the cantilever's falling moment as the set angle θ_p and the foot's offset δ_p (closed forms, checked by quadrature), the offset moving the spring's rest across (the return mapping, 60 bisections), so the moment stays at the section's capacity while it yields; parting by strain when K_t·κ·c passes ε_b, at m* = f − (f − 1)·(κ_c·K_t·c/ε_b)² (nominal 2.98 N·m, the set then about 6°); the T-joint's skid slip (2μP_t, 500 N) and socket (6.13 N·m), either `SkidLoose`. All + − × ÷ √; 0 B a step (`TheLawAllocatesNothingInAStep`, 2000 steps through a landing that bows, cracks, sets and breaks). A broken or detached leg's state latches and its offsets stay; its parts keep their contacts until 1.3 (the stub, the loose piece). The clamp's twist and the skid's slip have no degree of freedom of their own: the twist's turn rides the corner's springs across (a rotation of their rests about the axis), the skid's slip is its onset and state only.
