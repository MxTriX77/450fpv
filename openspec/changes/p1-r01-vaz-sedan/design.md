# Design: p1-r01-vaz-sedan

## Context
The pipeline after `p1-h01-adobe-house`: generator-written mask atlases under `textures` roles, composed kit pieces, micro meshes, shells, impostors, cylinder colliders, `rubber` and `fabric` in the material table (`rubber` 0.70 / 0.60, 5e4 N/m; `fabric` 0.40 / 0.30, 160 N/m per touching part, `p1-materials-per-part`). The sheet gives the published 2101 dimensions, M's measured damage (hole sizes and halos, the flap 0.35–0.45 × 0.6 m, the 0.05 m corner drop), the shared car collision plan, the kit items, the F drop derivation (0.282 − 0.183 + spring sag = 0.15–0.20 m, default 0.17), the colours and the `vehicle` table. Labels below are for the numbers this design adds.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** the M car in A and the same car burnt, from two params files; the `vehicle` generator and its kits for R02–R09; torn steel as geometry the fiber can cut on.
**Non-Goals:** see proposal; no interior detail beyond seats, dashboard, steering wheel and door trims; no engine.

## Decisions
1. **Profile stations.** The asset-artist derives the 2101's `side_profile` (10 stations: front bumper, bonnet front, bonnet rear / cowl, windscreen top, roof front, roof rear, rear screen base, boot front, boot edge, rear bumper; (x, z) in metres) and `plan_profile` (half-widths at those stations) from published outline drawings of the model, labelled derived in the preset, with `belt_line` 0.9 m, `sill_height` 0.3 m and `tumblehome` 0.12 m (assumed from the class ranges). The shell is lofted through them (`lib/loft.py`: a closed section per station from the sill to the belt line, the greenhouse above with the window openings cut from the sheet's sizes), panel thickness 0.8 mm rendered with its edge at every opening (a double-sided shell with the edge faces, assumed as the cheapest real-thickness build). Doors, bonnet and boot lid are cut from the shell along the published panel lines and rebuilt as separate parts so the kits can move them.
   *As derived (task 1.1):* the source is the VAZ 2101 side, front and top outline drawing at https://www.the-blueprints.com/blueprints/cars/lada/3472/view/vaz_2101/ (its full-size 923 × 503 px image; one drawing of three views, the only public one found with a readable scale), scaled to the sheet's published sizes (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VAZ-2101 · https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/ВАЗ-2101 · https://auto.ru/catalog/cars/vaz/2101/specifications/razmer-koles/). Its axle lines give 6.47 mm/px at the published wheelbase; at that scale its length reads 4.072 m and its tyre 0.589 m (published 4.073 m and 0.594 m), but its roof only 1.327 m, so heights are scaled by 1.44 / 1.327 to the published unladen height (the sheet's open point, settled at M). The overhangs read 0.615 / 1.034 m (derived; with the wheelbase they make the length). Its top view is scaled to the published width. The preset carries 12 stations, the 10 above plus `bonnet_mid` (the plan's front-wheel flare: 0.787 m against 0.766 m on a straight nose taper) and `rear_screen_top` (the roof's rear curve, which a straight segment to the rear screen's foot misses by 14 cm). From the front view, derived: `belt_line` 0.935 m, `sill_height` 0.344 m, `tumblehome` 0.055 m, the drip rail at 1.355 m, the shoulder's roll radius 0.10 m and the roof's edge roll 0.055 m.
2. **Details.** Bumpers: boxes with rounded ends and two overriders (`steel`, chrome recipe); grille: a mesh plane with an alpha-clipped mesh texture (derived); lamps: hemispherical bowls with a chrome bezel ring; wheels: a tyre torus (solid, tread as a normal map), a steel rim with a domed hubcap; interior: two front seats without headrests, a rear bench, a dashboard box, a steering wheel torus Ø 0.39 m; the rack: 20 mm tubes on four posts 0.10 m above the roof, 1.3 × 1.0 m. Plates: `none`.
   *As built (task 1.1):* `plate` takes `none` or `blank`; R01 is `blank`, as the proposal says (SEC-02: never a character): two plain plates of the standard Ukrainian 520 × 112 mm (published: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Ukraine), 1 mm sheet, the front one on the bumper blade's middle, the rear one on the rear panel over the bumper, whose plate lamp sits on the bumper (published: https://www.vazbook.ru/en/01/2101/main/system/fonari-vnutrennego-i-naruzhnogo-osvescheniya); heights assumed from the bumpers; white #d9dad6 (assumed, dusty) as a fourth column of the `car_lamps` palette, slot `plate`, `sheet_metal`; inside the bumper's and tail's collision. The grille is geometry, not an alpha-clipped texture: 15 vertical and 3 horizontal chrome bars over a dark backing (REAL-05).
3. **The A kit** (M): `frag_holes` 16 on the burst side (6 of 0.08–0.25 m with petals bent inward 10–30 mm and `edge` snags; 10 of 10–30 mm cut in the mesh at ≥ 30 mm, else in the mask), `rust_halo` 1.8, `bonnet` corner_folded with `bonnet_flap` 0.45 m standing against the windscreen (a hinge line along the torn corner, assumed), `windscreen` crazed_holed (the hole where the flap's edge meets it, assumed 0.1 × 0.2 m), `glass_side` gone on the burst side with crumbs as a micro mesh inside and below, `flat_tyres` the front burst-side wheel (the sidewall folded: the torus squashed 0.05 m at the ground, assumed as a scaled lower half), the body leaning by the derived 1–2° about the opposite diagonal, `months_abandoned` 6 (dust B, streaks R), `debris_count` 12 of the three K07 pieces within `debris_radius` 3 m at 1/r² density, `overgrowth` 0, `ground` soil.
4. **The F kit:** `ride_drop` 0.17 m (derived in the sheet), the tyres replaced by two bead-wire rings per wheel (torus r 3 mm, `steel`, `hook`) on the ground and round the rim, `rims_melted` false (steel wheels), hubcaps gone, chrome dulled (the chrome recipe's smoothness 0.25, assumed), `burn` 1.0 from `burn_origin` engine (mask A: burnt-off paint over the engine bay and cabin roof, soot G elsewhere), `weeks_since_fire` 4 (grey-white oxide, a little rust), glass gone, `interior` burnt_frames (seat frames as `steel` box frames with zigzag springs as hooks, the steering wheel's core), `roof_push` 0.04 m sag with a crease, doors closed (no cabin gap), `soot_patch` 4 m as a flat disc mesh 2 mm above the ground with `burned_ground_01` and a feathered alpha (assumed as the G1 stand-in for the terrain's soot mask), `ground` soil, `debris_count` 0 (no strike fragments at G1).
5. **Masks.** The generator writes the vehicle damage mask in asset space (one 2K atlas per variant: R rust, G soot, B dust, A burnt-off) under `textures` role `damage_mask`, and a crack mask per crazed pane (1k, radial and concentric lines from 1–3 impact points, assumed line widths 1–3 px) under role `crack_mask`. The paint recipe layers paint (tinted #2f4a40 with fade 0.3), rust (R), soot (G), dust (B) and burnt-off steel (A) in the facade-style layered shader of H01 extended with the A channel (tech-artist's call whether one shader serves both; the recipe schema is the same).
6. **Glass** (tech-artist, `CrazedGlass.shader`): URP transparent, slight green tint, the crack mask as an opacity and normal perturbation, double-sided; the pane geometry is 5 mm thick with the hole cut (REAL-05).
7. **Collision** (the shared plan): three body boxes (front, cabin, rear; following the plan taper), thin boxes (0.02 m) for bonnet, boot lid and roof (landable), A/B/C pillar capsules r 0.045 m, the crazed windscreen as a 5 mm box (`glass`), four wheel cylinders (`rubber` in A, `steel` at the rim radius in F), bumper boxes, interior boxes (`fabric` seats in A, `steel` frames in F), the steering wheel as a thin cylinder, the flap as a thin box at its angle (not landable), the rack as four capsules r 0.01 m on four post capsules; 28 primitives in A (derived from the list), 30 in F with the springs folded into the seat-frame boxes. Fragment holes ≤ 0.25 m stay closed in collision (the sheet's stated deviation). Gaps: none. Wind: lower body box porosity 0, greenhouse box 0.35 (A) / 0.55 (F). Snags per the sheet. Landable: roof (the rack rails are the touch points: the roof box top is set at the rail height with a note), bonnet except the flap's corner, boot lid.
   *As built (task 1.1):* the clean base holds **84 primitives, not 28**. The build's audit (`lib/audit.py`) holds every visual face within 2 cm inside 0.3 m of a landable top, and the roof, bonnet and boot lid put most of the car's upper half in that zone; a box over the shoulder's 0.10 m roll leaves its corner 41 mm off the skin (r(√2 − 1)), and the roof's 0.055 m edge roll 23 mm, so the rolls are cylinders, the greenhouse's leaning bands boxes per side and stretch, and the rear arches open octagons (the rear tyres stand 5.6 cm inside the body side, past the 5 cm a closed face allows). The roof is three landable boxes on the flat crown and the rack's side rails are landable capsules (not the roof box raised to the rails). The groups: lower body 15, wedges and lids 4, rolls 10, greenhouse 23, wheels and front wheelhouses 6, bumpers 10, interior 8, rack 8.
8. **LODs** (935 px/rad): LOD0 under 60 000 triangles (assumed: the shell with its edges, holes and interior); LOD1 at 20 m (holes under 0.08 m closed, interior as boxes, 1 mm edges dropped); LOD2 at 80 m (a hull with the greenhouse openings); LOD3 at 300 m (a box hull); cull 1 500 m. Textures: paint, rust, soot, chrome 1k (derived: a 4 m car at 256 px/m is 1k), the damage mask 2K.
9. **K07 pieces** (`debris_car`): `K07_lamp_bowl` (a hemisphere Ø 0.17 m, `steel`), `K07_bezel_ring` (a torus Ø 0.18 m, chrome, `steel`, `edge`), `K07_trim_plate` (0.35 × 0.2 × 0.001 m, `steel`, `edge` 0.5 mm), `K07_bead_wire` (torus Ø 0.37 m, wire r 3 mm, `steel`, `hook`); glass crumbs: 10 × 10 × 4 mm cubes as a micro mesh (the sheet's 3 600 per side window; capped at 1 000 per window at G1, assumed) with a `glass` note in the handoff.
10. **Params files.** `assets-src/vehicles/R01_A.json` and `R01_F.json` from the shared table with `model` R01, `body` sedan, `front` 2101, `roof_rack` true, `vent_panes` true, `wheel` steel_hubcap, `paint` dark green, `state`, `severity` 0.5, `burst_dir` from seed, and the kit items of decisions 3 and 4; ranges and effects in `PARAMS`; `check` refuses `state` B, `pose` other than upright, `front` other than 2101 and `model` other than R01 ("not built yet"), and a hole larger than the panel it sits on.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- [The profile stations are derived from drawings, not measured] → labelled derived; the M comparison (the roof line against the wheel, the sheet's open point) settles the height.
- [A double-sided shell with real edges doubles the triangles] → LOD0 only; LOD1 drops the edges; the budget is checked.
- [Rust, soot and burnt-off in one layered shader shared with houses] → the tech-artist decides at 1.3; the recipe does not change either way.
- [The soot patch as a disc mesh] → a G1 stand-in for the terrain's feathered soot mask (phase 3); stated in the handoff.
