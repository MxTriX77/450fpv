    **As built:** `Fpv450/CrazedGlass` is one premultiplied-alpha forward pass (URP/Lit's "Premultiply" mode for glass), cull off with a back face lit from its own side, no depth write, no shadow caster (clear glass passes the sun). The reflection stays whole: F0 from the recipe's `ior`, rising towards grazing as URP's environment term does. The body adds its scatter times its opacity, and the alpha is 1 − (1 − opacity)(1 − reflected), so the background gets only what the pane neither reflects nor stops. The crack mask (R, linear, clamped) draws lines of edge-on glass colour (#a9bdb4, the sheet's) at 0.9 coverage, and a groove one texel deep whose slope tilts the normal, so the lines catch the sun; mips average sub-pixel lines into a haze.
