# Design: p1-v01-locust

## Context
The pipeline after `p1-v13-shrub`: `lib/skeleton.py` (multi-stem bases, tapered tubes, two branching orders), `lib/cards.py` (atlas cards, LOD halving, the crossed far cards), alpha-clipped two-sided recipes, the shell rule in the audit, `bark` and `twig` in the material table. The sheet §V01 gives: heights 6 / 10 / 14 m, dbh 0.08 / 0.16 / 0.26 m, stems 2 / 1 / 1, first fork 2.5 / 3 / 4 m, main limbs 3, orders 5, children per order 2.2, fork angle 25–40°, sinuosity 0.4, crown widths 3 / 5 / 6 m, lean 3° (edge 8°), leaflet 2.5–5 cm, leaf 25 cm with 13 leaflets, pods 7 cm, bark #6e6559 / #7a5a44 grooves, leaf summer #4f6a2e, autumn #d4c25a, canopy at distance #5d6457 (H) and #70716d–#796e66 (F); porosity 0.55 / 0.75 / 0.9; the gap rule (openings ≥ 0.5 m written; ≥ 0.706 any yaw, 0.574–0.706 nose-first). Labels below are for the numbers this design adds.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** the tree generator every tree reuses; a locust that matches F, H and G; the belt's far look at 200 m.
**Non-Goals:** see proposal; no procedural bark geometry (the furrows are the set's normal map; the trunk's silhouette is sinuous geometry), no roots.

## Decisions
1. **Skeleton growth.** `tree.py` drives `skeleton.py` with: `stems` trunks from a base disc, each a sinuous tapered tube (`trunk_sinuosity` as a lateral random walk per metre, seeded), `main_limbs` at `first_fork_m` ascending at `fork_angle_deg`, then `orders` of children with `children_per_order`, length and diameter halving per order (assumed: the usual allometry, Leonardo's rule for the section), ending in zigzag twigs of 3–10 mm with a 15° kink per 0.1 m (assumed from the published zigzag). The trunk is 12-sided at LOD0, limbs 8, branches 6, twigs 3; LOD1 drops the twigs and halves the sides; LOD2 keeps the trunk and main limbs; the impostor follows.
2. **Cards.** Leaflet cards from the LeafSet assembled into pinnate leaf cards (one card per leaf of 25 cm, 13 leaflet cells laid along a rachis in the atlas space; derived: assembling at card level keeps the card count near V13's budget), placed along the twigs of the last two orders with an outward bias; `leaf_cover` scales the count (0.9 summer, 0.6 late summer, 0.3 autumn with the yellow tint, 0 bare); pods as 2-card strips on 20 % of twig tips when `fruit_share` is set. LOD0 of `V01_L` under 40 000 triangles (assumed budget: a belt of 50 L trees in view at 10 m stays under 2 M, with LODs doing the rest), halving per LOD.
3. **Impostor** (`lib/impostor.py`): headless Blender renders the LOD0 tree with its materials from 8 azimuths at 15° elevation (assumed: belts are seen near level or from a little above) into a 1k atlas with alpha, flat-lit with the leaf and bark albedo so the gallery's light shades the card; the importer builds a 2-card cross (or an 8-card octahedral set if the tech-artist later asks) whose UV picks the nearest view; switch at the distance where the crown width drops under 24 px (derived: 6 m at 935 px/rad → 230 m for L; 190 m for M; 115 m for S), cull at 2 500 m (assumed: the horizon band of A and L). The 6 % luminance check between the last mesh LOD and the impostor at 50 m (spec) guards the "dark band" look.
   - *As built in task 1.2* (asset-artist; `lib/impostor.py`, `build.py`, `lib/meta.py`, `CatalogImporter.cs`, `ModelPostprocessor.cs`, `Shaders/Impostor.shader`; checked on V13_mid, V01_M and V01_L with uncommitted `impostor: true` overrides at 120, 190 and 230 m; times not performance claims). *Bake:* params ask with `impostor: true` and `lod.impostor_switch_m`; after the FBX, staged like it, the build renders `<variant>_impostor.png`, 1024 px RGBA: the 8 views at 15° (azimuth k·45° from +Z towards +X) in a 4 × 2 grid of 256 × 512 px cells, one scale for all, every view 8 px inside its cell (a texel still at mip 3, assumed), the asset's vertical axis at each cell's centre; headless Cycles on the CPU, 32 samples, seed 0, no denoiser, Standard view, no dither, ancillary PNG chunks stripped (V13_mid's atlas came out byte-identical across five builds); flat albedo, each slot's base map as the importer picks it times its tint, linear as Unity takes it (`Mathf.GammaToLinearSpace`, a 2.2 power above 1: 1.252 → 1.639568, measured in Unity). Two more renders of the same views measure what one albedo card cannot carry: the crown's *occlusion* (the base maps times the derived mask's G, which URP/Lit applies to ambient light only, over the atlas in luminance: V13_mid 0.907, V01_M 0.783, V01_L 0.730), put on the card's ambient light; and the *up share*, the lean of the card's normal at which its mean sun response over suns at 30, 45 and 60° and 8 azimuths (assumed) equals that of the normals the views saw (0.014, 0.108, 0.051). Render 8–17 s per variant. *Card:* one quad (2 triangles) that `Fpv450/Impostor` turns about the vertical axis to face the camera (the light, in the shadow pass) with the nearest view, instead of a static 2-card cross, whose crossed cards show edge-on; its normal per pixel is an ellipsoid's round the leaf cards' vertices (centre, horizontal and vertical radii; an upright cylinder's above and below, where trunks stand), on the camera's side, leaning by the up share: an ellipsoid's visible normals spread as leaves turned every way do, where the leaf rule's horizontal normal left side-lit V01 a third dark (measured); matte (at the leaves' 0.2 the card's Fresnel rim put the overcast tile 8–12 % over LOD0, measured); receives no shadow, casts facing the light. *Metadata and import:* the impostor is the last LOD (`<variant>_impostor`, 2 triangles, its switch, and the record: atlas, size, views, grid, elevation, card, crown, `inputs_sha256`, `atlas_sha256`); `meta.validate` holds it last before the cull, at most 1024 px, one quad, and the FBX check leaves it out; an atlas whose two hashes match is not rendered again (its measures stand), a hand edit is rendered over, params that stop asking remove it. The importer puts the card mesh and material beside the prefab, imports the atlas as an sRGB diffuse keeping its coverage at 0.5, and sets the LOD group's size and reference point back to the metadata's after `SetLODs`, which takes them from the renderers' bounds (V13_mid's card would have pushed every switch 2.3 times out, measured). `build_asset.ps1` derives textures before the Blender build, which now reads the derived maps.
   - *Measured, the review's 50 m tile* (a throwaway editor script forcing each LOD; crown pixels from renders over black and white, ground and bar hidden; luminance of the coverage-weighted mean linear colour; impostor against LOD0 / against the last mesh LOD): V13_mid day 0.0589 against 0.0722 (−18 %) / 0.0907 (−35 %), overcast 0.1124 against 0.1115 (+0.8 %) / 0.1585 (−29 %); V01_M day 0.0593 against 0.0645 (−8.1 %) / 0.0558 (+6.3 %), overcast 0.1036 against 0.0988 (+4.9 %) / 0.0946 (+9.6 %); V01_L day 0.0543 against 0.0665 (−18 %) / 0.0531 (+2.1 %), overcast 0.0999 against 0.0980 (+1.9 %) / 0.0932 (+7.2 %). So the 6 % check passes for V01_L by day only. Overcast the card holds its LOD0 within 5 %; by day it is 8–18 % dark (the matte card has no sun highlights, and one up share cannot follow the crown's normals for every sun). And the last mesh LODs stray from their own LOD0: V13's crossed far cards, built from the brightest sprig cores, are 26–42 % over it with 43 % more crown pixels; V01's LOD2 (every fourth leaf) is 13–20 % under it by day with 43–52 % of its crown pixels, so it would pop into the impostor at the switch.
   **Amended after task 1.2** (orchestrator): the colour check compares every LOD with LOD0, not the impostor with the last mesh LOD, because 1.2 measured the last mesh LODs themselves off LOD0 (V13's far cards 26–42 % bright, V01's LOD2 13–20 % dark at half the coverage) and a pop at any switch is the defect; the impostor gains a 1k normal atlas, since one albedo card with one up share stays 8–18 % dark in sun and the extra atlas is the cheapest physically right fix (fidelity over simplicity, CLAUDE.md). Tasks 1.2a (the measure) and 1.2b (the fixes) carry it.

4. **Collision.** Trunk: capsules per 1–2 m following the sinuous centre line (radius at the mid-height, within 5 cm); limbs and branches ≥ 10 mm: one capsule per straight segment; the generator prints the count (`V01_M` expected 150–300, the sheet's assumption; if it passes 300, segments of 10–15 mm fold into the shells, the sheet's own fallback, and the handoff says so). Shells: `twig` pieces (spheres and capsules) fitted per main limb by V13's shell module with the cap by steps (the fewest of 24 to 64 per fit; a limb split by its child subtrees when 64 do not pass), audited per tree under rules (a)–(d) with a 0.35 m card allowance (the 0.25 m leaf card plus 0.1 m); twig tubes ringed at every kink at most 0.1 m apart, with polyline records; the gap search runs after the fit, bounded by capsules and pieces (`review-physics.md` §5, PR1–PR7). Gaps: the generator searches the crown for inter-limb openings by sampling planes between neighbouring limbs and keeps every opening with an inscribed circle ≥ 0.5 m as a polygon with `narrowest_m`; the handoff lists them with any-yaw or nose-first. Wind: the trunk column (box of width dbh, porosity 0) from the ground to the base of one crown box over the tree's twig, leaf and pod vertices, porosity by state (autumn 0.3 reads 0.825, derived from the sheet's assumed points); the two never overlap, because the wind grid multiplies the porosities of overlapping volumes and per-limb boxes would overlap (PR8). Snag: the sheet's rule as V13 builds it (`p1-v13-shrub` review-physics PR3–PR4): every capsule segment under 30 mm an `edge` at its radius, the top segment of each free member a `hook` to its tip, and one record per twig (`edge` where twigs leave its end, `hook` where it is free). Landable: none.
5. **Materials.** Bark: `bark_willow` or `bark_brown_01` at 2k (derived: a 0.8 m circumference L trunk at 512 px/m needs 410 px around; 2k keeps the furrows at 1.5 m) tinted #6e6559 with the orange groove tint applied in the derived albedo by the set's cavity, and the wound mask channels left black (V07's); leaves: one LeafSet at 2k with the summer tint #4f6a2e and the autumn lerp; pods: a derived 2-card strip painted by script (two brown lobes, assumed).
6. **Params files.** `assets-src/vegetation/V01_{S,M,L}.json` from the sheet's preset over the shared tree table, with `seed`, `height_m`, `dbh_m`, `stems`, `first_fork_m`, `main_limbs`, `orders`, `children_per_order`, `fork_angle_deg`, `trunk_sinuosity`, `crown_shape`, `crown_width_m`, `lean_deg`, `leaf_cover`, `autumn`, `spines`, `fruit_share`, `impostor`, `lod` { switch distances, `cull_m` }; ranges and effects in `PARAMS`; `check` refuses a first fork above the height and a crown width outside the sheet's row.

7. **The H autumn state** (added after `p1-v13-shrub` task 1.3 compared V13 with H). Per-variant materials: the catalog gains an optional `variant_materials` map; the importer resolves a variant's slot through it first. Autumn recipes: `leaf_shrub_autumn` and `leaf_locust_autumn`, the same atlases with the sheet's autumn tints (V13: honeysuckle yellow; V01: pale yellow #d4c25a lerped with the summer green to the mixed look H shows), and the same leaf shader. Variants: `V13_{low,mid,tall}_autumn` at `leaf_cover` 0.4 (assumed inside the sheet's autumn 0.5 and bare 0.1, judged against H) with the stems and twigs unchanged, so they show through; `V01_{S,M,L}_autumn` at 0.3 (the sheet's autumn). The H comparison needs a view at 1–1.5 m height, 5–10 m out, with clumps in front of trees: the review stage holds one asset, so the look task renders the row with a small throwaway editor script (or notes the limitation) and the G1 gallery's belt station is built in the H state. The task-1.3 measurement of band fill (rows against the flat overcast ground) is reused.

8. **Inherited from V13's QA** (`p1-v13-shrub` review.md, re-review). N-1: the shell fitter hits its 24-piece cap on V13_tall at sparse autumn cover, so task 1.0 sets the cap for crowns and sparse cover before task 1.1 fits V01's crowns and before task 1.4 builds the autumn variants. N-2: a twig snag record whose point lies outside every shell piece takes its contact material from the twig slot, not from an enclosing shell (for the phase-7 compiler). N-3: draw shell pieces in their own overlay colour, apart from stem capsules, in the review sheet when V01's crowns make the overlay crowded. **Settled by task 1.0** (`review-physics.md`): the cap is per neighbourhood (rule (d): 64 pieces of one asset per 2 m cube, 96 over the map), sparse cover keeps the rules and takes more pieces (`V13_tall` 40 at `leaf_cover` 0.4, 60 at 0.3, never below 0.3), and every snag record carries the material of the member it follows (N-2), so V13's metadata is re-emitted with that field.

9. **As built in task 1.1** (asset-artist; `review-physics.md` PR1–PR8). Times are plain Python and headless Blender on the dev machine while another agent's sixteen worker processes held its cores; the power state was not recorded, so they are not performance claims.
   - *Sets* (decision 5). Bark: `polyhaven/bark_brown_01` at 2k (1 × 1 m, published), chosen over `bark_willow` by look: its ridges run up the stem and interlace in the locust's diamonds, where `bark_willow` is a dark scale running diagonally across the tile. Its yellow-brown ridges (AO at or above its median 148: linear (0.228, 0.154, 0.060), measured on the 2k diffuse) take the sheet's grey-brown through the tint. Leaves: the pinned `ambientcg/LeafSet002` serves, so no leaf set is pinned. Each of its six cells is a sprig of 12–20 small oval leaves on a central stem; a whole cell on the 0.25 m leaf card reads as the pinnate leaf, the stem its rachis and the leaves its leaflets at 2.5–6.8 cm (V13 decision 3 measured them at 0.10–0.27 of a cell; the sheet's leaflets are 2.5–5 cm, so the largest run long). A card maps one cell, so the leaflets need no assembling in the atlas. Pods: painted by `derive.py` (`paint`): two flat pods with their seeds' bulges from one stalk, 128 × 256 px, #5c3d28 (assumed: ripe locust pods are a dark reddish brown), rims at 0.7 of it.
   - *Recipes.* `bark_locust`: tint [0.845, 0.928, 1.252], the sRGB-encoded linear factors (0.683, 0.843, 1.671) that take the ridges to #6e6559; the grooves tinted in the derived base map (`groove`): the weight runs from 0 at the AO's median 148 to 1 at its 10th percentile 65, and the grooves (AO 65 or under: linear (0.101, 0.064, 0.020), measured) take a further (2.833, 1.91, 1.716) so they render #7a5a44; 1.2 % of those texels clip in red (measured). The wound mask (R scars, G char, B flaking) stays V07's: bark_locust carries none, so nothing is derived or read for it. `leaf_locust`: LeafSet002 with the tint [0.642, 0.742, 0.688], which takes its opaque texels' mean #7f9147 to the summer #4f6a2e (the same measurement gives V13's committed [0.709, 0.676, 0.663]); smoothness 0.2 as V13's; clipped at 0.5 and two-sided, so the leaf shader. `pod_locust`: the painted base, clipped and two-sided, smoothness 0.2, no normal map. The importer takes a `groove` or `paint` recipe's derived base map (EditMode `DerivedBaseRecipeTests`).
   - *Skeleton* (decision 1, `lib/skeleton.py` `trunks` and `branches`). The trunk tapers linearly from 1.1 × dbh at the ground (assumed: the root flare) through dbh at 1.3 m, in 1–1.5 m segments, to the first fork exactly; a clump's stems stand on a 0.15 m disc and splay 5° more than the lean (assumed). The limbs share the fork's section by Leonardo's rule (weights 0.7–1.3, assumed) and run 0.8–1.0 of the crown's height over the fork (assumed). Every member of an order under `orders` carries 2 or 3 children (mean `children_per_order`) between 0.2 and 0.95 of its length at golden-angle azimuths, leaving at the fork angle; a child starts at half its parent's base diameter (±20 %), the design's halving. As built against the design: a child's length is 70 times its base diameter (`SLENDERNESS`, assumed: a 10 mm branch 0.7 m, a 5 mm twig 0.35 m), so it halves with the diameter, but at most 0.6–0.95 of its reach to the crown envelope, instead of half its parent's length; wood turns up 5° a metre (the ascending limbs, assumed); every member ends where it would leave the envelope; the axes wander as a random walk (`trunk_sinuosity` × 20° per joint × √step). Halving the envelope-clipped parents' lengths starved the crown: the first V01_M grew 75 of its 126 members and stood 7 m tall (measured). Wood under 10 mm at its base is a twig: a 3-sided zigzag of steps of at most 0.1 m, each swinging 15° the other way, a ring at every kink (PR6). Wood of 10 mm or more is 12-sided (trunk), 8 (limbs) or 6 (branches), a capsule per segment, and closed at both ends by domes of its end radii with two rings, its capsules' own caps (the base buried in its parent or in the ground, the trunk's top the crotch the limbs leave).
   - *Leaves and pods* (decision 2, `lib/cards.py` `along` and `hanging`). A pinnate leaf every 5 cm from a fifth of the way along each member of the last two orders (assumed: the shoot's internodes), alternate sides, leaving at 45–70° (assumed), facing out from the crown's axis as V13's sprigs do; `leaf_cover` is the share of those members that carry leaves (the sheet: the share of twig tips). Pod strips (two crossed 0.08 m cards) hang from the `fruit_share` of the last order's tips; these part-bare variants carry none (the sheet: pods in the autumn and bare states). LOD1 keeps every other leaf and pod strip and LOD2 every fourth leaf, unscaled as V13's LOD1; the far look is task 1.2's impostor.
   - *Shells* (PR1, PR5). `lib/shell.py` fits at 24, 32, …, 64 pieces and keeps the first fit that holds (b) and (c), else the 64-piece fit; the mass and the candidates are built once and the measured standoffs are shared between the steps. V01's part-bare crowns brought one more case: a bare twig's mass is a thin tube that the candidate lattice (every other 5 cm cell) misses, so no cap covered it (measured: V01_M limb 1 left a twig vertex 221 mm out with 18 pieces at cap 64). A geometry target that no lattice candidate reaches now gets a sphere of its own and capsules to its like within 0.6 m; a mass with no such target keeps exactly the lattice's candidates, which V13's three shells show by coming back piece for piece. Each main limb's twig, leaf and pod faces are one fit; no limb needed the split into its child subtrees (the split is tested with a stubbed fit).
   - *The audit.* Rule (c)'s allowance is the generator's stated card reach plus 0.1 m (`card_reach_m`: the shrub's `card_m`, the tree's `leaf_m`), rule (d) counts exactly (a sweep over the boxes' low corners), and (b) and (c) bin the pieces. A chain of capsules on thick wood showed two things V13's 18 mm stems never did: its caps, buried in the next capsule, stood up to 78 mm off the mesh at the trunk's joints and 22–37 mm on the limbs within 0.3 m of the inter-limb gaps (measured), and the nearest-face scan over 3 000 bark faces ran for minutes. The primitive -> mesh direction now measures a primitive's surface outside the other body primitives, as rule (a) does for the pieces (a soft shell piece hides nothing), on the shell rule's face grid with ties to the lower face index; K01's, V13_low's and V13_mid's audit lines come back identical (117 lines).
   - *Records, gaps, wind* (PR4, PR6–PR8). Every record names its member's material: a wood segment under 30 mm an `edge`, every wood member's top segment a `hook` to its free tip (children leave at 0.95 of it at most), each on its capsule's axis at its radius in `bark`; every twig one `hook` polyline through its rings at its mean radius in `twig`. Gaps: every two members of 30 mm or more leaving one fork (a clump's stems from the ground, the limbs at the first fork, a branch and its parent), in the plane of their first headings, between their surfaces offset 2 cm, closed by the chord at the first child fork of either and drawn in until no 2 cm sample inside lies within 1.5 cm of a capsule, shell piece or twig record (so no point 1 cm inside meets one), written at 0.5 m or more; the offset makes a written opening 4 cm narrower than the clear space between the two surfaces. Wind: one crown box over the twig, leaf and pod vertices, its porosity piecewise linear through the sheet's 0.9 / 0.75 / 0.55 at `leaf_cover` 0 / 0.6 / 0.9 (0.825 at 0.3, N-g); per trunk a solid box of width dbh along its chord from the ground to 1 mm under the crown box.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- [The capsule count against the world-query budget (K§10.5)] → printed per variant; the sheet's fallback folds 10–15 mm segments into the shells; the world-builder reads the count.
- [Gap search in a random crown finds many small openings] → only ≥ 0.5 m are kept; the count is printed; the gap probe of step 8 flies them.
- [Blender renders in a headless build add minutes] → one atlas per variant at 1k; `impostor: false` skips it for a quick rebuild.
- [Cards read as cards at 1.5 m under a tree] → the comparison against F at 1.5 m decides; the leaf shader with translucency stays the fallback (tech-artist).
