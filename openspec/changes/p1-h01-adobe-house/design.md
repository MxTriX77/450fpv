# Design: p1-h01-adobe-house

## Context
The pipeline after `p1-v01-locust`: ranges, the audit with shells and micro meshes, `derive.py` (ground recipes, masks), alpha-clipped recipes, impostors, cylinder colliders. The sheet gives every number (§Shared, §H01, §K02, §K03): footprint 10 × 6 m, walls 2.4 m to the plate and 0.45 m thick, ridge 5.0 m (pitch 38°), rafters 0.10–0.15 m at 1.0 m, battens 50 × 50 mm at 0.33 m, tiles 0.36 × 0.22 × 0.014 m with a nib and a 70–90 mm head lap, chimney 0.5 × 0.5 m to 0.6 m above the ridge in 250 × 120 × 65 mm bricks, door 0.8 × 1.9 m (plank leaf with two ledges and a brace, 0.10 × 0.08 m frame), windows 0.8 × 1.0 m (sill 0.9 m, frames 60–70 mm, 2 × 3 panes), eaves 0.4 m, base band 0.42 m, the main beam 0.20 × 0.25 m with 0.15–0.20 m joists and boards, rooms 2.2–2.6 m high; the colours (#c4bfb1 whitewash, #97754f render, #7d6146 adobe, #8a4a33 band, #a0613f tiles, #825f44 shards, #866c4d timbers, #7a5647 door, blue interior); the failure order; the rubble rule (removed solid volume × 1.86, split 40 / 35 / 20 / 5 %, spread exp(−d / λ) with λ 1.0 m tiles, 1.5 m bricks, 2 m timber); the D and X items; the gap verdicts. Labels below are for the numbers this design adds.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** the E house in I, D and X from three params files; walls that never chequerboard; openings whose clear space is the gap; rubble made of the kit pieces; a house generator the other five houses and the outbuildings reuse.
**Non-Goals:** see proposal; no interior furniture, no yard, no slate or sheet-metal roofs (the `roof_cover` branches raise "not built yet" in `check` until H02 and H04).

## Decisions
1. **Walls in asset space.** Each wall is built from its own origin (its outer-face bottom-left corner as seen from outside), u along the wall in metres, v up; corners continue the u coordinate across the wall junction by offsetting the next wall's u origin, so a 1.4 m tile never restarts at a corner (knowledge §14.4). Reveals take the wall's UV folded into the opening. The wall mesh is a box per segment between openings at LOD0 with the reveals and the crumble as geometry; the adobe blocks show only in the breach edges (250 × 400 × 200 mm block geometry at the jags, assumed as the sheet's crumbled edge).
2. **The facade material** (tech-artist, `FacadeLayered.shader`, URP lit): three tiled sets in wall space (whitewash `white_stucco_02` at 1.4 m, render `worn_cracked_plaster` at 2 m, adobe `rough_plaster_brick_02` or `Plaster006` at 2 m, each with the sheet's tint) chosen by the damage mask R (0 whitewash, 0.5 render, 1 adobe, with 0.1-wide feathered thresholds, assumed), soot G darkening, dirt B as streak darkening, A the base band (#8a4a33 tint over the render). The interior uses the same shader with `blue_plaster_weathered` as the whitewash layer. One material per house, its mask atlas per variant. Alternative rejected: one unique 2K albedo per wall (soft at 1.5 m, and four maps per house).
   *As built (task 1.2): no `FacadeLayered.shader`.* The facade and the interior draw with R01's `Fpv450/LayeredSurface` (R01 design 5's note) in the car's recipe format: each layer covers w = saturate((channel − threshold) / feather + 0.5) of what lies below it, in order. `facade_h01` and `interior_h01` keep their own material, the bare adobe (what a slot draws before its mask exists), and list a `base`, the whitewash (`whitewash`, the interior's `whitewash_blue`), the mask's `channels` (`render_depth`, `soot`, `dirt`, `base_band`) and five layers:
   - `clay_render` by R at 0.25 and `adobe` by R at 0.75, both feathered 0.1, so R 0 is whitewash, 0.5 render and 1 adobe;
   - between them `base_band` by A: paint over the render and under the bare adobe. It is the render's set and mask tinted to #8a4a33, so the render's cracks run on under the paint;
   - `wall_soot` (#2a2622) by G and `wall_dirt` (#4d3f31) by B, one-colour palettes on top.

   A channel's value is its layer's cover, so soot's and dirt's strengths are the mask's peak G and B (the generator's). The importer gains two optional recipe keys: `base`, and `channels`, which a mask record that names its channels must match, or the build is refused.
3. **The mask atlas** is written by the generator (`lib/masks.py`) into a 2048 × 1024 atlas (assumed: four walls and two gables at about 100 px/m on a 10 m wall, enough for 0.5–2 m patches): render loss as seeded patches around openings, corners, the foot and the head (`render_loss`), whitewash loss inside the remaining render (`whitewash_loss`), soot plumes 0.5–1.5 m above `soot_openings`, dirt streaks under sills and the eaves, the base band to `base_band_height`. The metadata lists it under `textures` with role `damage_mask` (additive key; schema 1).
4. **Roof.** The plate, rafters at `rafter_pitch`, ridge board, collar ties, hip rafters at the hipped end, battens at `batten_pitch`; every tile a cambered mesh (12 triangles at LOD0, assumed) hung on its batten with the head lap; ridge and hip tiles half-round; the chimney through the slope in brick with K01's cell UVs. `roof_strip` removes tiles in seeded clusters biased to the eaves and the lee end (`tile_cluster_bias`); `batten_break` breaks spans with K03's splinter builder; `rafters_broken` drops rafters off the plate (the fallen ones become K03 rafter pieces inside).
5. **Joinery.** The door: five boards, two ledges, a brace, strap hinges, hung inward at `door_leaf_state` (hanging: 70° from the jamb on one hinge, assumed) with its own box; the frame four boxes. Windows: double frames of 65 mm, two casements with 2 × 3 glazing bars; `window_state` gives glazed (I: empty frames, glass as `glass` shards in the corners), shards, frame_empty, sash_hanging, frame_gone; the clear polygon is the opening minus the frame (70 mm a side) and any hanging sash.
6. **Interior.** The сіни at the door and two rooms from the footprint (assumed layout: hall 2 m, rooms 4 m and 4 m), inner walls 0.25 m (assumed), the main beam across, joists at 0.8 m (assumed), boards; `interior_ceiling_down` drops boards and clay as K03 boards and plaster slabs (0.3–1 m flakes, assumed) on the floor; `hanging_cables` 1 in D as a `cable` catenary from the ceiling.
7. **Rubble.** `rubble_volume` from the removed solid volume × 1.86; the heap as a sloped core mesh (flat-faced blocks, `masonry`, one sloped box per metre of foot) and the pieces scattered by exp(−d / λ) through `lib/rubble.py` using the kit builders (`lib/debris_tile.py`, `lib/debris_timber.py`; adobe blocks from the wall module), wider on the side away from the burst (`burst_azimuth_deg`, assumed param); piece counts printed. D: the tile heap 0.2–0.4 m deep, 1–2 m wide along the foot; X: 1–2 m inboard, 1 m outboard. Every piece carries its own box and snag.
8. **LODs** (derived from 935 px/rad): LOD0 everything (under 150 000 triangles for D, assumed: about 1 000 tiles at 12, battens and rafters, the pieces); LOD1 at 25 m (a 14 mm tile edge under 1 px): tiles merged into one strip per batten row with the same silhouette, pieces merged per material, openings kept; LOD2 at 100 m: walls and roof planes with the openings cut, the chimney; LOD3 at 400 m: a hull with the roof; cull 1 500 m (assumed: a village at the horizon of A).
9. **Collision** as the sheet: wall boxes per segment, plate, rafters, battens (0.05 × 0.05), tile rows as thin boxes, the chimney, the leaf, frame members, interior timbers, the heap's sloped boxes, every piece. Materials `masonry` (adobe, render, tiles, chimney, heap, plaster), `timber`, `glass`, `cable`. Gaps: the door, each window by its state, each roof bay whose battens are gone (0.9–1.1 m × the slope length; a bay with any batten in place is not a gap), the gable breach and wall breaches as their real polygons after jags, `narrowest_m` by the inscribed circle; the audit's 2 cm near-zone applies at every opening. Wind: walls (porosity 0), the roof field at `roof_strip` × (0.7–0.85) + (1 − `roof_strip`) × 0 per slope, tile clusters 0, the interior cavity as one box with porosity 1 marked `cavity` (assumed field: the wind model's sheltered volume), the door and the far window as its openings. Snags: `edge` on batten ends, rafter tails, the chimney cap, the leaf's edge, frame remnants; `hook` on the cable and on nails. Landable: none.
10. **Kits.** `debris_tile`: `piece` tile_shard (a seeded outline of 2–6 fracture lines through a tile, 14 mm, one box), tile_whole (cambered, two boxes), ridge_piece and the slate pieces range-checked but raising "not built yet". `debris_timber`: batten, board, rafter_sawn with `break_ends` and K03's splinters (capsules ≥ 10 mm, `hook`), nails as 3.5 mm hooks; beam, rafter_round, panel, log_section raise "not built yet". Materials: tiles `clay_roof_tiles_02` with a derived fracture-face map; timber `raw_plank_wall` or `rough_wood` with grain along the piece and a fracture-end map; `masonry` and `timber`.
11. **Params files.** `assets-src/houses/H01_I.json` (`damage` 0, `render_loss` 0.3, `whitewash_loss` 0.3, `roof_strip` 0.03, `window_state` glazed with shards), `H01_D.json` (`damage` 0.55: `roof_strip` 0.75, `batten_break` 0.3, `rafters_broken` 2, `door_leaf_state` hanging, `window_state` frame_empty with crumbled edges `opening_jag` 0.2, `wall_breaches` one on the back wall 0.9 × 1.2 m, `shard_heap_depth` 0.3, `interior_ceiling_down` 0.45, `hanging_cables` 1), `H01_X.json` (`damage` 1.0: `roof_strip` 1, `collapse` the gable end and the back long wall to 1.0 m, the chimney to a stump, `rubble_volume` from the rule); the shared table's keys with ranges and effects in `PARAMS`; `check` refuses a door or window outside AST-04 for `wall_material` adobe, a batten pitch that passes the drone, and a `roof_cover` other than clay_tile.
12. **Letter S's items in the shared generator** (D-038, the S read-in's `implications.md` (archive/2026-10-19-p1-letter-s-village-street)). H01 stays E's copy, but the `house` generator it writes carries what the typical village's house needs, so H02 variant 3 is params: `ceiling_debris` (0–1, the share of the roof's debris resting on an intact or holed ceiling instead of the ground: S 45–50), `tv_mast` (position and height, standing above the stripped roof: S 50), `frame_paint` as a shared item with white among its values (frames that stay in their openings, sashes and glass gone: S 47–50), and an X state whose `collapse` takes every wall down to 0–0.8 m with the masonry stove left standing (bonded brick about 1 × 0.8 m and 1–1.5 m high with its flue, the one upright thing in the footprint: S 17–18). Each defaults to H01's E values, so H01's three states are unchanged by them.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- [150 000 triangles at LOD0 with every tile] → LOD1 at 25 m merges tiles into strips; the pilot's close look is one house at a time.
- [Hundreds of collision boxes per house] → the sim reads the metadata through the world query's spatial index (phase 3); the count is printed and the physics engineer reads it.
- [The facade shader is new tech-artist work on lane B's critical path] → its task runs in parallel on a sub-branch and touches only `Shaders/`; until it lands the importer binds the adobe set alone, so the generator tasks are not blocked.
- [Tile size and profile are assumed from the batten pitch] → the sheet's open point; settled by G1, not by the pilot.
- [Four agents on one change] → each task is one logical unit (kits, the intact house, the damage states, the look); the generator is written to be extended, not rewritten, by the damage task.
