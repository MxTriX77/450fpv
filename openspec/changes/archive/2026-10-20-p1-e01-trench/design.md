# Design: p1-e01-trench

## Context
The pipeline after `p1-s04-belt-straw` and `p1-l01-l03-pole-wires`: `lib/ground_patch` (a gridded patch with relief, mounds, the S04 material, the straw kit baked as micro meshes), the `ground` recipe kind with a detail layer, micro meshes skipped by the audit, `surface:<id>` on shapes, the surface framing of the capture, `ports`, `wires`. The sheet §E01 and the group's shared rules give every number: lip 0.8 m, floor 0.6 m, depth 1.5 m, batter 86°, berm 0.3 m, parapets 0.45 m (field side) and 0.3 m (belt side) high on 2.5 m bases at 22° (derived from a 1.05 m²/m cut × 1.25 swell), bends 25° every 5 m (G), niches 0.8 × 0.6 × 0.7 m one per 10 m, topsoil band 0.5 m, lip crumble 0–0.1 m, colours #4a463f (inside, shade), #b49c6a (parapet straw), #a88f70 (subsoil band), the hole contract F-1…F-8 (on 0.5 m cells the band runs 0.65 m past the cavity's widest outline and the backing 0.75 m past the band's edge, as the cellar C01's), the width-at-height table. Labels below are for the numbers this design adds.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** the G trench on a self-contained patch, with fillers and gaps the physics will read unchanged, and a `holes` record the map compiles later.
**Non-Goals:** see proposal; no heightfield of its own (the patch is a mesh), no blending into neighbouring surfaces (the patch's margins end at its edge; the gallery floor meets them).

## Decisions
1. **Patch and run.** `run_m` 24 (assumed: three G bends and both niches fit; the 10 m tile shows the whole run), the centre line a polyline from `shape` curving with `bend_deg` every `bend_every_m` 5 (G) on arcs of `bend_radius_m`, seeded jitter ±5° and ±`leg_jitter_m`; the patch is the run's bounding box plus `margin_m` 6 on every side (derived: the parapet base 2.5 m plus the berm plus 3 m of plain ground), 35.25 × 13.75 m for `E01_curving`, with S04's relief and the grassed-spoil cover. Origin at the first centre-line point, y = 0 the ground. **As built (task 1.1, sound by QA's review §7):** the patch is the trench's own grid, not `lib/ground_patch`'s (whose grid steps for S04's bare patches): 0.25 m cells, refined to 12.5 cm near the run and over the parapets and to 6.25 cm at the parapets' ends, so the grid follows the parapets' creases within 2 cm; the lip is cut exactly by the top slot's quads, so no 0.1 m lip cells are needed.
2. **The cut.** Per segment between centre-line samples (1 m straight, 0.5 m on bends, assumed so that planar wall faces stay within 5 cm of the curve): the floor at −`depth_m`, walls at `batter_deg` 86 from the lip width to the floor width, the lip crumbled, spade facets of 0.2–0.3 m only in the normal map; two niches cut into one wall at `niche_every_m` 10 (sill 0.15 m above the floor). The berm is level for `berm_m` 0.3 from each lip, then the parapet rises on a 22° slope to its height and falls on the far side; `age` 0.8 gives grassed parapets (S04's material, lodged) and a trampled floor; `age` 0 (not built) gives bare lumpy spoil (S03's look, assumed) and sharp lips. **As built (task 1.1, sound by QA's review §7):** the crumble is a per-side walk of the lip's inset over the samples, 1–10 cm and changing at most 3 cm a metre, which leans each wall's top back (a twisted wall within 1 cm of its box), not a 0.1 m jagged inset on 0.1 m cells, which boxes cannot follow within the lips' 2 cm; the lip reads crumbled through the mat's straws hanging over it in clusters (decision 11). **Task 1.6 (S8):** `width_jitter_m` 0.05 (the sheet's 0–0.15 m, spade-dug irregularity): each wall stands out by 0 to half of it, drawn per metre and linear between, floor and lip together, so the batter is kept and the cut is never narrower than its widths (assumed: widening only keeps the sheet's corridor verdicts everywhere); held over a niche's segment.
3. **Floor detail.** Spilled clods: `clods_per_m` 0.5–2 (assumed from G's floor reading lumpy), 0.1–0.4 m, as low irregular meshes with one `surface:crater_spoil` sphere each, within 5 cm; blown-in straw and twigs at a tenth of S04's densities (assumed) as micro meshes from `lib/straw`, so the audit skips them.
4. **Fillers.** Per segment: a floor box 0.3 m thick under the floor plane; two wall boxes 0.3 m thick behind each wall face, rotated to the batter, from the floor to the lip; two lip boxes 0.3 m thick under the berm, level (F-3); each extends 0.1 m past its segment's ends so neighbours overlap 0.2 m (F-5) and the niche walls get their own three boxes; every cell the bands can mark is sealed from below by a filler (F-1, a 5 cm ray grid over the bands and 0.71 m past their edges: a cell marked by its centre reaches at most 0.354 m past a band, one marked by touch 0.707 m, so the lip boxes and the backing run 0.75 m past the band's edge, under the berm and the parapets' inner slopes and never above y = 0), and every cavity face, the crumbled lip and the niches included, stands at least 0.65 m inside the union of the bands (F-2: 0.25 m plus half a 0.5 m cell's diagonal, 0.354 m, rounded up, so it stays 0.25 m inside the hole cells at any placement yaw whichever way a cell is marked); nothing above the patch's ground outside the cut (F-4: the lip boxes top at y = 0). Materials: `surface:belt_bare` on every cut face (the sign-off's F-7 row), not landable. Parapets: per segment one tilted box per slope face (rotated 22° about the run direction, 0.3 m thick) and one flat box for the crest, `surface:belt_straw`, landable; margins: the patch's flat box from S04 (12 m-style, here the patch's extent), `surface:belt_straw`, landable. The audit's near-zone rule (2 cm at landable faces) applies to the lips and parapets: relief there is held to ±2 cm (assumed; S04's ±4 cm holds only on the plain margins). **As built (task 1.1, sound by QA's review §7):** the lip boxes run at full depth, from the lip line down to the block's bottom and out to 0.75 m past the bands, so they are the backing as well (F-1 on every grid; each at least 0.3 m in every dimension); the margins are columns 2 m wide (`surface:belt_straw`, landable, 5 cm seams, stopping 1 m from the centre line inside the lip boxes' reach), not one flat box. **Task 1.6 (S4):** the berm is grassed ground: under it a berm box 0.3 m thick, `surface:belt_straw` and landable, its top the berm at y = 0, over the lip box (whose top drops to −0.3 m there); both reach 0.15 m past the parapet's inner toe, under its slope, and at least 0.3 m from the lip; beyond the toe the lip box keeps its top at y = 0 under the parapet, so the fillers' top stays level at the bands' edges (F-3). Every cut face stays `belt_bare`, every box 0.3 m thick behind its face. **Task 1.6 (S3):** `lib/holes` holds every joint to its own 0.2 m (F-5), whatever the generator's constant; the generator reports its joints. **Task 1.7 (S13):** a joint is every interior point of a hole's centre line, and a generator that reports none, or misses one, fails the build. **Task 1.7 (R3):** past each run end the berm turns round the end as level grassed ground, so it gets the berm's boxes: the end block's top drops to −0.3 m under a 0.3 m end berm box (`surface:belt_straw`, landable) over the block's whole footprint, and round the band's end a ring of 8 filler boxes (22.5° each, 0.3 m across inward from the band's edge, 0.3 m tall) tops 25 mm under that box, so F-3 still finds a filler within its 30 mm at the band's edge (assumed: 5 mm to spare); along the sides the lip box beyond the toe starts 5 cm inside the berm box's reach, where the slope already stands 4 cm over its top, and the walls' and end walls' boxes top 3 mm under the lip (a wall's twist stood a corner 0.8 mm proud). So outside the opening every top a contact meets first is `belt_straw`, and no `belt_bare` top lies within 1 mm of it.
5. **Gaps.** A cross-section polygon at every centre-line sample and at every bend vertex: the clear trapezoid from the floor to the lip in the plane normal to the run; the top slot per segment: the quad between the two lips at y = 0. `narrowest_m` is the inscribed circle (0.75 m for the default section, derived: the circle touches both walls where the width equals its diameter), and the design records that the drone's verdicts come from the sheet's width-at-height table (nose-first everywhere, yawed in the top 0.7 m), which the handoff states and the gap probe of step 8 flies. Gap ids carry the chainage (`xs_12.0`, `slot_12`) so the probe's log names the place. **Task 1.6 (R2, S5):** the open top is one slot per stretch of about a metre, independent of the segments (every slot lies at y = 0, so nothing asks for it finer): the run in equal stretches, as many as fit at least as long as the widest opening the params allow plus 5 cm (21 of 1.143 m for `E01_curving`), each slot's vertices on the straight lip lines, so the slots tile the opening and each one's inscribed circle is the opening's width (0.914 m and more), never a short arc piece's length; and each niche's mouth is a gap in the wall's plane, `mouth_<chainage>`, 0.8 × 0.6 m (`narrowest_m` 0.603). **The corridor as built (S2, accepted):** square to the run, the floor 0.612–0.643 m wide (19–34 mm a side for a 0.574 m drone nose-first), 0.677–0.716 m at 0.35 m (52–71 mm), the lip 0.891–0.975 m (158–200 mm); yawed 45° (0.706 m) the drone fits from the lip down to 1.00–1.20 m below it. QA measured before the width jitter 13 mm at the floor, 43–54 mm at 0.35 m, 141–186 mm at the lip and 0.88–1.04 m yawed: the crumble leans the whole wall back (batter 82.4–85.8°, inside the sheet's 80–90°), so the bends are turnable yawed deeper than the sheet's 0.7 m; PR-9 holds.
6. **The `holes` record** (`lib/meta.py`, additive, schema stays 1): `{"id": "run", "centre_line": [...], "top_width_m": 0.8, "depth_m": 1.5, "band_width_m": 2.35}` (derived: the lip at its widest, 0.8 m plus the 0.025 m width jitter and the 0.1 m crumble a side, plus 0.65 m a side, C01's margin; 2.3 m before task 1.6's jitter), and one record per niche in the same form: its centre line square to the run from the trench's centre line to the niche's back wall, `top_width_m` 0.8, `depth_m` 1.35 (the niche's floor), `band_width_m` 2.1 (derived: a niche's cavity lies under the ground up to 0.7 m past the wall, and ground over a cavity must be a hole, as over C01's chamber, or the terrain contact takes the drone inside it for a penetration; the asset's own ground and the niche's ceiling box carry the ground there). A record's band is every point within `band_width_m` / 2 of its centre line in plan. The map compiler (phase 3) rasterises the band into `holes.png` and lowers nothing (the fillers carry the cut); the parapets become heightfield in the world (F-7), which the compiler reads from the asset's mesh there, not from this record. **As built (task 1.1, sound by QA's review §7):** the ids are `run`, `niche_0` and `niche_1`, unique per asset (the validator holds them so); the compiler namespaces them per placement.
7. **Materials.** `trench_wall.json`, kind `ground`, in wall space (u along the run, v down the wall): base = the chosen loam set under `brown_mud_02`, tinted to #4a463f, with a wall mask (the topsoil band #33302b in the top `topsoil_m` 0.5, a root fringe in the top 0.2 m, clod spill #8a7660 in the bottom 0.2 m) composed into a strip along the run (as built, task 1.1: 27 × 4 m at 2048 × 1024, 76 × 256 px/m, rows for the floor, the field wall and the belt wall, the end walls past the run; the tiled detail layer at 512 px/m carries the grain), and a spade-facet normal (0.2–0.3 m facets, < 2 cm) derived by script; the floor uses the same recipe's floor row (trampled, #4a463f). The parapets and margins: `belt_straw` as is (superseded by task 1.4: used as is it stamps S04's bare-patch ovals every 12 m with no relief under them; the parapets get their own grassed-spoil recipe, the physics staying `surface:belt_straw`).
8. **LODs.** LOD0 everything (grid, clods, micro); LOD1 without micro, as built (task 1.6, S1) from 24 m, since Unity measures the switch from the 35 m patch's centre, so that the review's 1.5 m and 10 m views (10–12 m from it) see LOD0 and every straw is at least S04's 8 m off when LOD1 comes (decision 11); LOD2 at 60 m the patch at 1 m with the cut as a plain slot; cull 400 m (assumed, as S04). Budget: under 60 000 triangles at LOD0 for the ground, the lip refinement and the clods (derived: the 0.25 m patch is 15 000, the lip refinement about 5 000, the clods under 2 000), plus the lodged-mat micro bake under 450 000 as S04's (S04 design 4: a stand-in for AST-10's near ring; amended after QA's R1, task 1.6), drawn on the LODs the review's 1.5 m and 10 m views use.
9. **Review capture.** `ReviewCapture`: for catalog class `earthworks` with a `holes` record, the pilot-height camera stands 1.5 m above the lip at the first centre-line point (offset 0.4 m to the field side), looks along the run's first segment and 20° down (assumed: G flies along the trench at a low height), with the bar planted 3 m along the run beside the cut; the pose is printed. K01 and S04 captures stay pixel-identical.
10. **Params file** (`assets-src/earthworks/E01_curving.json`): `seed`, `shape` curving, `run_m` 24, `bend_deg` 35 (25 at first; 30 by task 1.2, 35 by task 1.5), `bend_every_m` 5, `bend_radius_m` 3.7 and `leg_jitter_m` 0.75 (task 1.5), `top_w_m` 0.8, `bottom_w_m` 0.6, `width_jitter_m` 0.05 (task 1.6), `depth_m` 1.5, `batter_deg` 86, `berm_m` 0.3, `parapet_h_m` {field 0.45, belt 0.3}, `parapet_base_m` 2.5, `parapet_slope_deg` 22, `niche_every_m` 10, `niche_m` {w 0.8, h 0.6, d 0.7}, `lip_crumble_m` 0.1, `topsoil_m` 0.5, `age` 0.8, `clods_per_m` 1, `margin_m` 6, `revetment` none, `collapse` 0, `overgrowth` 0, `lod` { 8 (24 as built, task 1.6), 60, 400 }; every key with a range and an effect in `PARAMS`; `check` refuses a floor wider than the lip, a batter under 75°, and parapet volume that does not match the cut's swell within 30 %.

11. **The lodged mat (task 1.6, R1; G 3, 6, 9: one continuous combed mat over the parapets, the berm and the lip).** Task 1.5's hummocks and lip tongues are gone; the mat is S04's kit (`lib/straw`) baked as S04 bakes its straws, a visual-only micro mesh of LOD0 (`mat`), a stand-in for AST-10:
   - **Where:** over the berm and the parapets, from 1 cm off the cut's opening to the parapets' outer toe, thinning to none over 0.5 m past it (task 1.5's feather, carried); about 178 m².
   - **How many:** S04's two layers of lying straws, the sheet's 250 /m² over the body's 90 /m² (carried), 58 638 straws. They are straight tubes of one segment (6 triangles; **derived:** two segments would take 640 000 triangles over 178 m², past the 450 000).
   - **Gathered and lofted as S04's:** on the crowns of the relief the top carries (`clump` 0.9, **assumed** from G 3–6: the hollows left as the mat's dark crevices; S04's level mat takes 0.6), each end at its layer's share of a 6 cm loft (1 − clump of it in the hollows), never 7.5 cm over the ground, darker the deeper (S04's, carried).
   - **Combed:** 75 % within 20° of the local combing (**assumed** from G 3, 6), the rest crosswise: down the fall line, tip downhill, where the ground falls more than 11° (the parapets' 22° slopes, **assumed**: lodged grass on a bank), along S04's swathes on the level.
   - **Over the lips:** 240 straws a metre before a value noise on a 0.25 m lattice thins them, none where it falls under 0 (**assumed** from G 3, 6, 9; 4 471 straws, 90 a metre on average, about half of each lip bare: there the wall's dark topsoil shows under the mat's edge, G's notches). Each runs from the berm 5–30 cm back, out over the lip 0–6 cm past it and 0.4–3 cm up, then down the battered wall 3–25 cm (the long ones rarer) and 0.5–3 cm off it, its tip drifting along the lip, shaded a fifth darker; all **assumed** from G 3, 6, 9. Each lies in front of its wall, and nothing of the mat stands within the drone's 0.287 m half-span of the centre line below the lip (the nearest vertex below the lip stands 0.326 m from it, 0.293 m at the most the ranges allow).
   - **The map under it:** `grassed_spoil`'s cover takes the tone of the same relief in its hollows (`tools/assets/derive.py`: a cover may name its asset's relief, `patches.relief`, and the trench's `relief_inputs` give it), at S04's `RELIEF_TONE` 0.45 and `RELIEF_AO` 0.4 (carried). So the tussocks' crevices read at 10 m with the straws, and beyond LOD0 without them.
   - **LODs (S1):** the mat and the floor's straws are LOD0's; LOD1 is LOD0's ground without them from 24 m (**derived:** the farthest straw stands 15.6 m from the LOD group's reference point, the bounds' centre, so every straw is at least S04's 8 m off when they go; the review's 1.5 m and 10 m views lie 10–12 m from it and see LOD0). Drawn on LOD1 too, the sub-pixel straws aliased into a grey gravel band round the run at 50 m without MSAA, and the FBX doubled.
   - **The clods (S6):** they take the wall's own map at the depth they fell from (5–110 cm below the lip, **assumed**), never the foot's pale spill band.
   - **Budget:** LOD0 39 172 triangles of ground and the bake 411 276 (the mat 405 480: 351 828 lying, 53 652 over the lips; the floor's straws 5 796), under 60 000 and 450 000; LOD1 39 172; LOD2 1 835; the FBX 18.8 MB.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- [Overlapping filler boxes confuse the two-sided audit near the overlaps] → the audit measures open faces only; the design states which face of each box is open; the audit lines show it.
- [Tilted parapet boxes over a crumbled grassed slope] → the slope is planar per segment at 22°; the relief on it is held to ±2 cm (decision 4).
- [The strip map's 76 px/m along the run (as built, task 1.1) reads soft at 1.5 m] → the tiled detail carries the grain; judged against G, where the inside is in shade and reads uniformly dark.
- [The zigzag and straight shapes are untested] → range-checked only; built with E02–E04 later.

## Open Questions
- Which loam set (`dirt_floor` or `forest_ground_04`) reads as G's shaded walls: decided at the comparison sheet.
- **Settled: the band's margin against the map grid.** C01's rule, which holds whether the compiler marks a cell by its centre or by touch: every cavity face 0.65 m inside the bands, the backing 0.75 m past their edges, a band per niche (decisions 4 and 6). WLD-16's marking rule stays the world-builder's choice in phase 3 and changes nothing here. The berm stays its look value (`berm_m` 0.3): the asset draws its own ground, berm and the parapets' inner slopes over the hole cells, and the heightfield takes the parapets from its mesh outside them (decision 6), so a wider band widens no berm. The question as it was put (found by the cellar sheets' holes pass): a 0.5 m margin holds F-2 (cavity faces at least 0.25 m inside the hole cells) only when the run is aligned with the grid; at 45° with cells marked by their centre a face comes within 0.146 m of the cell boundary. Either the margin grows to at least 0.61 m (0.25 m plus half a 0.5 m cell's diagonal; C01 and C02 use 0.65 m), which takes the band past the shared rules' 2.0 m, or the compiler marks every cell the band touches and the backing fillers reach 0.71 m past the band. Settled with WLD-16's cell-marking rule (world-builder, phase 3) before task 1.1 writes the record; the record's field stays the same either way.
