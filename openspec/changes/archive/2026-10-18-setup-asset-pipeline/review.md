## Suggestions (non-blocking; 1 and 2 before the second asset)

1. **Guard the collision-follows-visual rule in the build, and the params in the generator** (failure B above). `debris_brick` should refuse a chamfer that cannot fit (`bevel_mm.max / 1000 ≥ min(size) / 2`), and `build.py` should run the two-sided deviation between each collision primitive and the LOD0 mesh it stands for (the physics review's P1-2, which QA's `k01_deviation.py` does for a box), failing beyond AST-05's ±5 cm (±2 cm near a gap or a landable face). Today a generator can write a collision box that stands for nothing, and the build says OK. Owner: asset-artist, before the second asset; physics signs off the tolerances.
2. **Validate before exporting.** Move `export.export_fbx` after `meta.validate` in `build_variant` (keep `check_against_fbx` after the export), or export to a temporary path and move it only when both checks pass, so a rejected build writes nothing, as the spec scenario says. Owner: asset-artist.
3. **K01 wears a wall, not a brick.** `brick_red` is the Poly Haven running-bond wall tiled at 1.4 m, so a single 250 × 65 mm brick face shows a 0.25 × 0.065 m window of a wall: mortar joints may cross the brick, and the brick format in that scan reads longer and flatter than 250 × 65 (about 4.5 : 1 against 3.85 : 1). For a loose brick the right material is a single brick face (albedo plus edge wear), and the 1.4 m tile size is an assumed number that the recipe doesn't label. Owner: asset-artist, with the K01 object sheet and the review sheet (`setup-review-capture`), judged against the reference letters before K01 enters the gallery.
4. **The FBX's own LODGroup.** Unity's `_LOD0/_LOD1` naming convention makes the imported model carry a LODGroup with Unity's default percentages (`lODScreenPercentages: [0.25, 0.01]` in `K01_whole.fbx.meta`). The prefab builds its own group from the metadata and references only the meshes, so nothing uses it, but anyone who drags the FBX into a scene gets Unity's distances, not the catalog's. Turning the convention off in `ArtImportSettings` (or asserting in the EditMode test that the prefab's heights come from the metadata) would close the gap.
5. **Cylinder colliders (physics review P1-1).** `EnsureColliders` throws on `cylinder`; resolve before the first pole or trunk asset (a convex `MeshCollider` or a capsule of the same radius and height, noclip-only).
6. **Perpendicularity vs planarity.** With `PERPENDICULAR_TOLERANCE = 1e-3`, a vertex a few millimetres off the plane of a small opening is refused by the perpendicularity rule before the 10 mm planarity rule applies (fuzz case `gap_vertex_8mm_off`). Fine for clean generators; if jagged openings need the full 10 mm, relax the edge rule to the planarity-implied angle, or define the normal from the polygon's best-fit plane. Owner: asset-artist with physics sign-off.
7. **Commit subjects over 72 characters** (three on this branch). Keep subjects within AGENTS.md's limit.
8. **Roughness and AO are imported but not wired** (design risk, recorded): the packed mask map is phase-1 work; until then `_Smoothness` is a constant per recipe.
