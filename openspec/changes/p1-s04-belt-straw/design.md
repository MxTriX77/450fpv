# Design: p1-s04-belt-straw

## Context
The pipeline after `p1-pipeline-hardening`: params ranges per generator (`PARAMS` + `check`), `lib/audit.py` (two-sided ±5 cm, ±2 cm near landable faces), `tools/assets/derive.py` (packed masks from a recipe), `surface:<id>` accepted on shapes, `CatalogImporter` (URP/Lit, mask wiring, box/sphere/capsule/cylinder colliders), the review capture (1.5 / 10 / 50 / 200 m tiles from the asset bounds, the stage ground at 0.18 grey). The object sheet §S04 gives every number: mat 5–20 cm, lying straws 0.3–1.0 m × 2–5 mm at 250 /m², standing stems 0.5–1.2 m at 60 /m² with seed heads on 30–60 %, twigs 5–20 mm at 2 /m², bare patches 0.5–2 m (8 % of area), green strips 1–3 m (15 %), mounds 0.3–0.5 m across, mean albedo #7a7055, straw #b49c6a, soil 10YR 2/1–3/1. All labelled there; the labels below are for the numbers this design adds.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** the H look at 0.3–1.5 m and at 10 m from one params file; a reusable `ground` recipe and `straw_kit` module; the surface's physics metadata as the sim will read it.
**Non-Goals:** see proposal. Also: no shader of its own (URP/Lit with a detail layer is enough for one patch; the phase-3 terrain shader reuses the derived maps), no impostor.

## Decisions
1. **The asset is a patch, not a tile.** `ground_patch` builds a 12 × 12 m patch (assumed: it fills the 1.5 m and 10 m views and the station floor; a 200 m view of a flat patch is a dot either way), origin at the centre, y = 0 the mean top of the mat, LOD0 a 0.25 m grid (derived: mounds 0.3–0.5 m across need at least two cells) with relief ±0.04 m (assumed: the mat's unevenness, under the audit's 5 cm) and `mounds_per_m2` mounds 0.3–0.5 m across and 0.08–0.15 m high (assumed: a fresh burrow heap) as raised cells. LOD1 is the same grid without the micro meshes, LOD2 a 2 × 2 quad. Alternative rejected: a 1 m tile with wrapping edges; it would show its repeat at 10 m and give no mounds.
2. **Materials: one unique base map, one tiled detail.** The `ground` recipe (`assets-src/materials/belt_straw.json`, kind `ground`) names `polyhaven/brown_mud_02` as the soil and the straw set as the cover; `derive.py` composes a 2K unique albedo/normal/mask for the 12 m patch (170 px/m, derived; the straws at the pilot's feet are geometry, so the base need not reach 512 px/m) from the two sets through the seeded patch mask (bare 0.08 at 0.5–2 m, green 0.15 in strips 1–3 m, lodging azimuth bias 0.3) and tints it so the mean lands on #7a7055; the cover set rides as the URP/Lit detail layer (`_DetailAlbedoMap`, `_DetailNormalMap`, keyword `_DETAIL_MULX2`) at the set's tile size (1.4–2 m, from the set). The unique base kills visible tiling at 10 m, the detail gives grain at 1.5 m. Alternative rejected: a two-layer custom shader (tech-artist work the phase-3 terrain shader will do once, properly).
3. **The straw set.** TextureCan Ground 0018 is the sheet's choice and its licence was read by QA (CC0). `fetch.py` gets a `texturecan` source only if a direct download URL exists per map at a fixed resolution; if TextureCan serves files only through a browser session, the asset-artist picks a CC0 straw or hay ground set from Poly Haven or ambientCG, records it in the sheet's material line and in the handoff. Decided at build, not here.
4. **The instance kit is a library module, the bake is a stand-in.** `tools/blender/lib/straw.py` builds the kit meshes (tapered tubes: `segments` 2–6 at LOD0, 3 sides, a seed head as a 4-triangle tuft; `variants` 4–16 per kind) from the sheet's table (`straw_len_m`, `straw_d_mm`, `straw_bend`, `stem_h_m`, `seed_head_share`, `twig_len_m`, `twig_d_mm`). `ground_patch` scatters them (seeded, Poisson-ish jitter on a grid, crosswise with the weak lodging bias) within `bake_radius_m` 4 m of the centre and returns them as a **micro mesh** of LOD0. Budget, derived: 50 m² × (250 + 60 + 2) /m² ≈ 15 600 instances; a 3-sided 4-segment tube is 24 triangles, so about 400 k triangles at LOD0 plus the grid; the FBX stays under 30 MB. LOD1 drops the micro mesh at 8 m (derived: a 3 mm straw subtends one feed pixel at about 2.8 m, 935 px/rad; 8 m leaves margin for noclip at 1080p). The phase-1 step-7 generator will place the same meshes at 0 mm parity with physics (AST-10); the bake is deleted then.
5. **Micro meshes in the contract.** The generator's return gains `"micro": [{"lod": 0, "name": "<variant>_LOD0_straw", "kind": "straw", "count": n}]`; `export.py` writes them as extra mesh objects under the LOD, `meta.assemble` lists them under a new `micro` key (empty array when none, schema stays 1: additive), `audit.run` excludes them from the LOD0 cells and prints `audit: skipped micro mesh <name> (<count> straw)`, `CatalogImporter` adds their renderers to the LOD and never a collider. Alternative rejected: a separate asset for the straws (it would stand nowhere without the patch and double the catalog bookkeeping).
6. **Physics metadata.** `collision`: one box 12 × 0.2 × 12 m, centre (0, −0.1, 0), `surface:belt_straw`, `landable: true`; one sphere per mound (radius from its width, centre sunk so the cap matches the raised cells within 5 cm), `surface:crater_spoil` (the physics sign-off's row for loose spoil and fresh mounds), `landable: true`. `gaps: []`, `wind_volumes: []`, `snag: []` with the deviation stated in the handoff and the review: the straw hooks are the micro-detail generator's (AST-10), not asset snags. The mat's own parameters (modulus, hook rates, wet rules) stay in the surface table; this asset only names the row.
7. **Review capture: a surface framing.** In `ReviewCapture`, when the catalog class of the asset is `surfaces`, the pilot-height shot is taken from (0, 1.5, 0) above the patch centre looking 35° down towards +X (assumed: the H frames look down at the mat from about chest height a few metres ahead), with the pilot bar planted 2.5 m ahead inside the frame; the log prints the pose. Everything else is unchanged, which the K01 re-capture proves pixel for pixel.
8. **Params file** (`assets-src/surfaces/S04_patch.json`): `seed`, `size_m` 12, `cell_m` 0.25, `relief_m` 0.04, `mounds_per_m2` 0.02, `mound_w_m` [0.3, 0.5], `mound_h_m` [0.08, 0.15], `bake_radius_m` 4, the densities `straw_per_m2` 250, `stem_per_m2` 60, `twig_per_m2` 2, the kit params of decision 4, `physics_material` `surface:belt_straw`, `mound_material` `surface:crater_spoil`, `lod` { `lod1_switch_m` 8, `lod2_switch_m` 60, `cull_m` 400 } (assumed: the station floor should not vanish inside the 200 m view). Every key has a range and an effect in `PARAMS`.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- [A 400 k-triangle static mesh in the gallery] → one station only; measured in the handoff; `segments` and `bake_radius_m` are params if it is too heavy.
- [The unique 2K base is soft at 1.5 m] → the straws there are geometry and the detail layer carries the grain; judged at G1 against H, and the recipe can go to a 2 × 2 split of 2K maps later without a generator change.
- [TextureCan has no pinnable URL] → decision 3; nothing else depends on the set's identity.
- [The audit's near-tolerance (±2 cm at landable faces) over an uneven mat] → relief is ±4 cm but the box top is the mean, so the audit measures within ±4 cm; the landable near-zone rule must treat a surface shape's whole top as a landing face and use 5 cm there (AST-05's ±2 cm is for the hard landing faces of objects); recorded in the audit's lines and judged by QA. If the audit cannot express it, `relief_m` drops to 0.02.
- [Baked straws pop at the LOD1 switch] → a G1 stand-in; the step-7 near-ring fade replaces it.

## Open Questions
- None that change the specs. The green-strip share (10–30 %) and the seed-head share (30–60 %) are set at the sheet's defaults and tuned by eye against H at build.
