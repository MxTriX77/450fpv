# Design: p1-s04-belt-straw

## Context
The pipeline after `p1-pipeline-hardening`: params ranges per generator (`PARAMS` + `check`), `lib/audit.py` (two-sided ±5 cm, ±2 cm near landable faces), `tools/assets/derive.py` (packed masks from a recipe), `surface:<id>` accepted on shapes, `CatalogImporter` (URP/Lit, mask wiring, box/sphere/capsule/cylinder colliders), the review capture (1.5 / 10 / 50 / 200 m tiles from the asset bounds, the stage ground at 0.18 grey). The object sheet §S04 gives every number: mat 5–20 cm, lying straws 0.3–1.0 m × 2–5 mm at 250 /m², standing stems 0.5–1.2 m at 60 /m² with seed heads on 30–60 %, twigs 5–20 mm at 2 /m², bare patches 0.5–2 m (8 % of area), green strips 1–3 m (15 %), mounds 0.3–0.5 m across, mean albedo #7a7055, straw #b49c6a, soil 10YR 2/1–3/1. All labelled there; the labels below are for the numbers this design adds.

## Goals / Non-Goals
**Goals:** the H look at 0.3–1.5 m and at 10 m from one params file; a reusable `ground` recipe and `straw_kit` module; the surface's physics metadata as the sim will read it.
**Non-Goals:** see proposal. Also: no shader of its own (URP/Lit with a detail layer is enough for one patch; the phase-3 terrain shader reuses the derived maps), no impostor.

## Decisions
1. **The asset is a patch, not a tile.** `ground_patch` builds a 12 × 12 m patch (assumed: it fills the 1.5 m and 10 m views and the station floor; a 200 m view of a flat patch is a dot either way), origin at the centre, y = 0 the mean top of the mat, LOD0 a 0.25 m grid (derived: mounds 0.3–0.5 m across need at least two cells) with relief ±0.04 m (assumed: the mat's unevenness, under the audit's 5 cm) and `mounds_per_m2` mounds 0.3–0.5 m across and 0.08–0.15 m high (assumed: a fresh burrow heap) as raised cells. LOD1 is the same grid without the micro meshes, LOD2 a 2 × 2 quad. Alternative rejected: a 1 m tile with wrapping edges; it would show its repeat at 10 m and give no mounds.
2. **Materials: one unique base map, one tiled detail.** The `ground` recipe (`assets-src/materials/belt_straw.json`, kind `ground`) names `polyhaven/brown_mud_02` as the soil and the straw set as the cover; `derive.py` composes a 2K unique albedo/normal/mask for the 12 m patch (170 px/m, derived; the straws at the pilot's feet are geometry, so the base need not reach 512 px/m) from the two sets through the seeded patch mask (bare 0.08 at 0.5–2 m, green 0.15 in strips 1–3 m, lodging azimuth bias 0.3) and tints it so the mean lands on #7a7055; the cover set rides as the URP/Lit detail layer (`_DetailAlbedoMap`, `_DetailNormalMap`, keyword `_DETAIL_MULX2`) at the set's tile size (1.4–2 m, from the set). The unique base kills visible tiling at 10 m, the detail gives grain at 1.5 m. Alternative rejected: a two-layer custom shader (tech-artist work the phase-3 terrain shader will do once, properly).
   **As built (task 1.1):**
   - The detail albedo is derived too (`<material>_detail_albedo.png`): the cover's diffuse at its own size with its linear mean moved to 0.5 per channel, because URP/Lit's "detail albedo ×2" multiplies the base, so a raw diffuse (linear mean 0.37 for the straw set) would darken the tinted base by a third and double its hue. Neutral, it adds grain only; the detail normal is the set's own pinned map.
   - The base map spans `size_m` centred on the asset origin: `_BaseMap` tiling 1/size with offset 0.5 (UVs in metres, the patch centred, decision 1). URP/Lit derives the detail UV from the base UV, so the detail tiling is size/tile_size_m. The tile sizes of a ground recipe are scalars (square sets).
   - The straw layer alone takes the tint that lands the patch's linear mean colour on the target, with the soil (10YR 2/1–3/1, as pinned) and the green strips (#6a7a3a) at their own sheet colours; a global pass then corrects clipping. Measured: mean linear RGB 0.194/0.162/0.092 against 0.195/0.162/0.092, luminance −0.1 %, bare 8.2 %, green 15.4 %; the straw gain is 0.40/0.50/0.93 (the hay set is brighter and yellower than a lodged mat; the base straw stands for straw plus the shadow between straws, so the geometry straws of task 1.2 should take the sheet's #b49c6a and the combined mean is re-checked against H in 1.3).
   - No period repeats inside the patch: the cover is tiled at its tile size and at tile × φ and the two are averaged, stretched 1 + bias along the lodging azimuth and rotated to it (the cover normals turn with it). The azimuth is degrees from +u towards +v, that is +X towards +Z of the asset. Patches are blobs (an ellipse and three satellites), feathered 0.1 m (bare) and 0.5 m (green), both assumed; the shares are measured on the stamped masks and recorded in the stamp.
3. **The straw set.** TextureCan Ground 0018 is the sheet's choice and its licence was read by QA (CC0). `fetch.py` gets a `texturecan` source only if a direct download URL exists per map at a fixed resolution; if TextureCan serves files only through a browser session, the asset-artist picks a CC0 straw or hay ground set from Poly Haven or ambientCG, records it in the sheet's material line and in the handoff. Decided at build, not here.
   **As built (task 1.1): TextureCan taken, no substitution.** The details page (`texturecan.com/details/190/`) links one zip per resolution at a static path (`/downloads/ground_0018/ground_0018_2k_krQ65t.zip`, 63.1 MB) that a plain `urllib` request with the fetcher's User-Agent fetches without a session (the host's Mod_Security refuses a bare `curl` without an `Accept` header; the fetcher's request is accepted). Two downloads gave the same SHA-256 (`bef43ce2…`), so it is pinned like an ambientCG zip: the archive and each extracted map (colour and roughness JPG, AO JPG, 16-bit OpenGL normal PNG). The terms page (read 2026-10-18) puts every texture under CC0 1.0, so the source records licence CC0 with the page. `--add texturecan:<n>` takes the page number, TextureCan having no API. The page states no physical size: `tile_size_m` 2.0 is **assumed** (the straws in the preview are 0.3–0.5 m long at that size, inside the sheet's 0.3–1.0 m). The soil set's size is 1.3 m (published by the Poly Haven API).
4. **The instance kit is a library module, the bake is a stand-in.** `tools/blender/lib/straw.py` builds the kit meshes (tapered tubes: `segments` 2–6 at LOD0, 3 sides, a seed head as a 4-triangle tuft; `variants` 4–16 per kind) from the sheet's table (`straw_len_m`, `straw_d_mm`, `straw_bend`, `stem_h_m`, `seed_head_share`, `twig_len_m`, `twig_d_mm`). `ground_patch` scatters them (seeded, Poisson-ish jitter on a grid, crosswise with the weak lodging bias) within `bake_radius_m` 4 m of the centre and returns them as a **micro mesh** of LOD0. Budget, derived: 50 m² × (250 + 60 + 2) /m² ≈ 15 600 instances; a 3-sided 4-segment tube is 24 triangles, so about 400 k triangles at LOD0 plus the grid; the FBX stays under 30 MB. LOD1 drops the micro mesh at 8 m (derived: a 3 mm straw subtends one feed pixel at about 2.8 m, 935 px/rad; 8 m leaves margin for noclip at 1080p). The phase-1 step-7 generator will place the same meshes at 0 mm parity with physics (AST-10); the bake is deleted then.
5. **Micro meshes in the contract.** The generator's return gains `"micro": [{"lod": 0, "name": "<variant>_LOD0_straw", "kind": "straw", "count": n}]`; `export.py` writes them as extra mesh objects under the LOD, `meta.assemble` lists them under a new `micro` key (empty array when none, schema stays 1: additive), `audit.run` excludes them from the LOD0 cells and prints `audit: skipped micro mesh <name> (<count> straw)`, `CatalogImporter` adds their renderers to the LOD and never a collider. Alternative rejected: a separate asset for the straws (it would stand nowhere without the patch and double the catalog bookkeeping).
6. **Physics metadata** (rewritten from `review-physics.md` §3 and PR1–PR8; the first version had one 12 m mat box). `collision[0]` is the soil: one box 12 × 0.3 × 12 m, `surface:belt_straw`, landable, its top one mat depth below the mat top (D = 0.125 m, carried from the sheet) and the slab mesh deepened to D + 0.3 m. The mat over it is object-id `straw` (490 N/m, derived at exactly D), landable, top at y = 0, bottom on the soil: per bare patch one box per edge of its convex step outline (6–8 vertices) plus axis-aligned fill boxes over the rest of the slab, about 85–103 shapes. Over each patch the mesh drops by D, vertically or within 0.05 m, to the soil floor. Mounds stay `surface:crater_spoil` spheres on the mat, kept 0.5 m from every patch window. The shape order is soil, fill, edge boxes by patch, mounds. `gaps`, `wind_volumes` and `snag` stay empty (the straw hooks are AST-10's). The patch layout is one seeded module shared by `derive.py` and the generator. Why `belt_straw` and not `belt_bare` for the floor: a bare patch is the moist loam under the mat exposed, not beaten belt-floor loam.
7. **Review capture: a surface framing.** In `ReviewCapture`, when the catalog class of the asset is `surfaces`, the pilot-height shot is taken from (0, 1.5, 0) above the patch centre looking 35° down towards +X (assumed: the H frames look down at the mat from about chest height a few metres ahead), with the pilot bar planted 2.5 m ahead inside the frame; the log prints the pose. Everything else is unchanged, which the K01 re-capture proves pixel for pixel.
8. **Params file** (`assets-src/surfaces/S04_patch.json`): `seed`, `size_m` 12, `cell_m` 0.25, `relief_m` 0.04, `mounds_per_m2` 0.02, `mound_w_m` [0.3, 0.5], `mound_h_m` [0.08, 0.15], `bake_radius_m` 4, the densities `straw_per_m2` 250, `stem_per_m2` 60, `twig_per_m2` 2, the kit params of decision 4, `physics_material` `surface:belt_straw`, `mound_material` `surface:crater_spoil`, `lod` { `lod1_switch_m` 8, `lod2_switch_m` 60, `cull_m` 400 } (assumed: the station floor should not vanish inside the 200 m view). Every key has a range and an effect in `PARAMS`.

9. **As built in task 1.2** (recorded by the orchestrator from the handoff):
   - The straws' material is a new `palette` recipe kind: `derive.py` writes a flat 32 × 16 map (straw #b49c6a from the sheet; twig #6b5a3e, assumed) and the kit's faces are UV'd into its columns. A texture-less tint would be refused by the capture's empty-stage guard, so a flat map it is.
   - The collision audit treats the primitives as a **union** (the part of a shape inside another is not measured), and a `surface:<id>` shape is a landing face over its whole top held at 5 cm, not 2 cm: the mat's ±4 cm relief passes without dropping `relief_m`. A face bucket index keeps S04's audit near 10 s; K01's numbers are unchanged.
   - The importer caps the LOD group's size and sets it after `SetLODs`, so an asset taller than the screen at its first switch distance (the 12 m patch at 8 m) switches at its metadata distances (8 / 60 / 400 m), with an EditMode test.
   - Measured: 15 683 straw instances (50.27 m² × 312 /m²), LOD0 387 686 triangles, FBX 13.1 MB. Open for task 1.3: the mounds carry the straw base map (the sheet calls them small earth mounds), and the baked straws end at a hard 4 m circle.

10. **As tuned in task 1.3** (REAL-03; the review sheet's 1.5 / 10 m tiles beside H 2, 5, 8, 12, 16, 20 and G 5 in the local `reference/_compare/S04.png`; every number inside the sheet's ranges):
   - **Mounds are bare earth** (H 1–4): the top triangles inside a mound's foot take a new `soil` slot, material `belt_soil` (the soil set tiled at 1.3 m, its tint 0.867 / 0.914 / 0.977 landing its mean on the bare patches' soil), so a mound reads as an earth heap, not a straw heap. Rendered overcast, mound and bare patch measure #575146 and #595146.
   - **The bake has no rim:** `bake_feather_m` 1.0 thins every kind to nothing along a smoothstep over the last metre; 12 144 instances (312 /m² × 38.6 m² of feathered area), LOD0 294 732 straw triangles + 6 014 slab, FBX 10.2 MB.
   - **Stems in tussocks** (H 3–9): uniform upright stems at 60 /m² read as a reed bed from the pilot's height, while H shows tussocks with stems leaning every way and much of the mat lodged. `stems_per_tuft` 10 within 0.15 m (assumed), each leaning 15–60° away from its tuft's centre (`stem_tilt_deg` 60, assumed from H 3–9); density 60 /m² and height 0.5–1.2 m unchanged; `seed_head_share` 0.45 → 0.35 (sheet 30–60 %); `variants` 8 → 12 (sheet 4–16).
   - **Straw shades** (H 12–21: pale, mid and dark straws side by side): the kit cycles pale #cdb685, the sheet's #b49c6a and dark #977f55 by variant (assumed around the sheet's value); their linear mean is #b59e6e, within 3 % luminance of #b49c6a.
   - **The base reads as straws over soil.** The hay set has no gaps; tinted down to the mat's mean it read as a grey-khaki carpet beside H's bright straws over dark gaps. The ground recipe's cover now takes `gap_share` 0.25: the soil shows in the cover's darkest gaps (high-passed at 5 cm, so the set's tile-sized shading never repeats; measured 21.7 %), and the straw layer takes a brighter gain for the same mean (0.40 / 0.50 / 0.93 → 0.47 / 0.60 / 1.06).
   - **Bare patches are ragged** (H 9–14): the feather is 0.3 m (was 0.1) and broken by the cover's straws and a 0.4 m value noise, so a patch is soil with lobed edges and straws over it, never a clean ellipse. The soil takes the recipe's new `soil.albedo_target` #433f37 (inside 10YR 2/1–3/1; the pinned set's own mean #50473a is browner than H's grey soil, H 12 and 14).
   - **Green strips are olive** (H 16, H 19–21): the assumed #6a7a3a read lime against the mat at 10 m; #7f8a4e sits between it and the H 16 feed value #959763.
   - **The detail layer is grey:** the per-channel neutralisation multiplied the hay set's weak blue by 4.2 and put blue and lilac flecks into the base; a grey detail keeps the grain without a hue.
   - Measured: the albedo's mean luminance 0.1637 against 0.1638 (−0.1 %), bare 7.8 %, green 15.5 %, gaps 21.7 %. No repeat: the normalised autocorrelation of the albedo's luminance (high-passed at 0.5 m) over every shift of 0.4–4 m peaks at +0.14 beyond 1 m, where the cover tiled plainly at 2 m scores +0.77 at 2 m and +0.88 at 4 m; the 10 m tiles show no pattern by eye.
   - Not matched, for the record: the feed's own saturation and contrast (the feed stage's work); H's near straws lie in locally parallel swathes, ours cross at random with the global 0.3 bias; the review's 35° look-down from 1.5 m shows more soil between straws than H's 0.3–1.5 m forward view; G's low warm sun is not a review light, so G is matched only for the mat's texture and colour family.

11. **QA's first pass and how it is answered** (`review.md` R1–R5). R1: the ground stamp must hash the formula's constants, so a tuned constant never leaves stale maps behind an "up to date". R2: the palette scenario now names the four columns built. R3: every number §10 added carries its label. R4: the bare soil patches had a physical role but were texture only (a leg would land on straw physics where the mat is visibly absent); they become geometry: the mesh steps down through the mat over each patch and a surface shape with soil physics follows it, the patch layout shared between `derive.py` and the generator from one seeded definition. The physics engineer first reads the surface metadata (the 5 cm surface-top tolerance the audit now applies, the mound spheres, how a bare patch is represented: its step, shape kind and surface id; the proposal named that reading and it had not run). R5: the look is improved rather than the scenario narrowed: the mat gets loft (heaped, layered straws, not one sparse layer), tussock relief that reads at 10 m as G's lumpy mat, straws shaded along their length, and green regrowth as blades over the strips, judged again against H and G; §10's "not matched" list carries what still differs.

## Risks / Trade-offs
- [A 400 k-triangle static mesh in the gallery] → one station only; measured in the handoff; `segments` and `bake_radius_m` are params if it is too heavy.
- [The unique 2K base is soft at 1.5 m] → the straws there are geometry and the detail layer carries the grain; judged at G1 against H, and the recipe can go to a 2 × 2 split of 2K maps later without a generator change.
- [TextureCan has no pinnable URL] → decision 3; nothing else depends on the set's identity.
- [The audit's near-tolerance (±2 cm at landable faces) over an uneven mat] → relief is ±4 cm but the box top is the mean, so the audit measures within ±4 cm; the landable near-zone rule must treat a surface shape's whole top as a landing face and use 5 cm there (AST-05's ±2 cm is for the hard landing faces of objects); recorded in the audit's lines and judged by QA. If the audit cannot express it, `relief_m` drops to 0.02.
- [Baked straws pop at the LOD1 switch] → a G1 stand-in; the step-7 near-ring fade replaces it.

## Open Questions
- None that change the specs. The green-strip share (10–30 %) and the seed-head share (30–60 %) are set at the sheet's defaults and tuned by eye against H at build.
