# Proposal: w10-play-maps

**Owner:** game-developer · **With:** tech-artist (the stills through the feed) · **Reviewers:** qa-engineer · **Requirements:** PRD-05, APP-02, APP-03, APP-04, OSD-01…04, FEED-00, PLT-06 · **VISION:** §2, §3 · **Decisions:** D-100 (this change), D-088, D-089, D-092 · **Plan:** `docs/handover-2026-11-12.md` §3, unit B · **Tier:** Standard

## Why
The pilot, after flying the farmland preview (their words in `docs/handover-2026-11-12.md` §2, item 1): "let's have "Play", and in play we now also have 2 maps - the one that's currently the Train would be "House", the farmland would be called "Village". Should be a fancy carousel, and each map should have their screenshot." D-100 records it:
- the menu's Train becomes **Play**, which opens a carousel of the maps, then the setup and the flight as now;
- **House** is the test range (map id `test-range`), which left the player in W3 3.1 and comes back; **Village** is the farmland;
- each card has a still through the analog feed with the OSD: House's frame shows the car, the fence and the house; Village's is exactly Explore's spawn frame;
- the stills are generated by a capture method and a pose per map, never by hand, and a still of the friend's world follows D-089.

Also, Train's menu subtitle still reads "flies the test range" while Train flies the farmland, which misled the pilot.

## What Changes
- **The menu:** Train → Play, with a subtitle that says what it does. Calibrate, Explore (review builds) and Exit stay.
- **The carousel:** House and Village as cards, each with its name, a line on what it is, and its still. Keyboard, mouse and the radio's sticks move between cards and choose; Esc goes back to the menu.
- **House flies again:** the test range's flight (its scene, its map world, its start points and its files in the player) comes back under Play → House, with the setup and the flight as they were before W3 3.1, at the current physics model.
- **Village** is the flight that Train opens today, unchanged.
- **The setup, the flight, R, the summary and "again"** stay on the map that was chosen.
- **The stills:** a capture method renders each map's still through the analog feed with the OSD, from a pose per map in a params file. They are rendered at build time into a git-ignored folder and carried into the player, so no render of the friend's world is pushed (D-089).
- **The smoke test** flies both maps from Play.

## Non-goals
- No change to the physics, the feed's look or the OSD's layout.
- Explore stays on Village (the farmland), as now.
- No new map, and no change to either map's content.
- No setup options beyond PRD-04's.

## Impact
- `Scripts/UI` (`MenuScreen`, a carousel screen), `Scripts/App` (`AppFlow`, `FlightScene`, `FlightWorlds`), `Scripts/Feed` (the still capture), `unity/Assets/_Project/Editor/Build.cs` (the test range's scene and files, the stills), the build settings' scenes.
- The pilot's build grows by the test range's scene and files.
- The branch is `unity/physics/physics-integration-2--w10-play`, cut from `--w3-preview` at 241145ea. It merges into `--w3-preview` for the pilot's next build, before w11-world-map (they overlap in `AppFlow`).
